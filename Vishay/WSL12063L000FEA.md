## Summary

The WSL is Vishay Dale's welded Power Metal Strip low-value resistor family for current sensing (30100, p. 1). The part number decodes as WSL1206, value 3L000 (3 mΩ; "L" marks mΩ below 10 mΩ), tolerance F (±1 %), packaging EA (lead-free tape and reel) (p. 1, Global Part Numbering). In the T1 it is the sense resistor of the BQ28Z610-R1 gauge in the two-cell battery path, at the top of the 1–3 mΩ range TI recommends (board ADR 0017).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Range:** the BQ28Z610-R1 coulomb counter spans ±100 mV (SLUSE07B §7.11, p. 7): ±33 A with 3 mΩ, above the 9.3 A BQ25798 IBAT_OCP and the 5 A fuse.
- **Dissipation:** 2.4 A (full output budget) → 17 mW, 7 % of 0.25 W; 3.2 A (worst continuous case, board ADR 0014) → 31 mW, 12 %; 5 A (fuse rating) → 75 mW, 30 %; 10 A for ≤ 1 s (BQ25798 discharge pulse) → 0.3 W.
- **Resolution:** 3.2 A gives 9.6 mV; light playback (≈ 0.11 A, est.) gives 0.33 mV, about 108 LSB of the 16-bit counter.
- **TCR:** ±150 ppm/°C is above the ≤ 50 ppm/°C guidance of SLUSE07B §11.1 (p. 27); over a 25 K board swing that is ±0.38 % of gain, accepted in board ADR 0017.
- **Stock (2026-10-24, FindChips and the JLCPCB parts API):** TTI 84,000; Avnet 24,000; Mouser 5,901 @ $0.593 (100), $1.24 (1); Arrow 4,000 + 3,526; Verical 4,000; LCSC C4264731: 10 @ $0.96 (thin). Second source WSL12063L000FEA18 (Mouser 11,084).
