## Summary

The WSL is Vishay Dale's welded Power Metal Strip low-value resistor family for current sensing, with 0.5–5 nH inductance, < 3 µV/°C thermal EMF, sulfur resistance by construction and AEC-Q200 qualification (30100, p. 1). The part number decodes as WSL1206, value 5L000 (5 mΩ; "L" marks mΩ below 10 mΩ), tolerance F (±1 %), packaging EA (lead-free tape and reel) (p. 1, Global Part Numbering). In the T1 it is the sense resistor of the BQ28Z610-R1 gauge in the two-cell battery path (board BOM v1.5 line 43).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Range:** the BQ28Z610-R1 coulomb counter spans ±100 mV (SLUSE07B §7.11), ±20 A with 5 mΩ: above the 9.3 A BQ25798 IBAT_OCP and the 5 A fuse.
- **Dissipation:** 2.4 A (full output budget) → 29 mW, 12 % of 0.25 W; 3.2 A (worst continuous case, board ADR 0014) → 51 mW, 20 %; 5 A (fuse rating) → 125 mW, 50 %.
- **Resolution:** 2.4 A gives 12 mV; light screen-off playback (≈ 0.1–0.2 A from the pack, est.) gives 0.5–1 mV.
- The SLUSE07B p. 1 schematic shows 1–10 mΩ; Figure 9-1 shows 1–3 mΩ for its 6 A protection defaults, which do not apply here (protection is in the pack).
