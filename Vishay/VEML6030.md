# VEML6030

- **Manufacturer:** Vishay Semiconductors
- **Function:** Ambient light sensor, 16-bit, I2C, with interrupt
- **BOM v1 lines:** 60 (faceplate ambient light sensor)
- **Package:** 6-pin surface-mount, 2.0 × 2.0 × 0.87 mm, transparent
- **Datasheet:** VEML6030, document 84366, Rev. 1.8 (28 November 2024), sha256 `46bc766f22f7…` in `manifest.toml`
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The VEML6030 is an ambient light sensor with a photodiode, amplifier, 16-bit ADC, I2C interface and a programmable threshold interrupt (84366 p. 1). In the T1 it sits on the touch faceplate, powered from FP_3V3, on I2C3 at 3.3 V (VCCIO5) at address 0x10, with its open-drain INT routed to SoC GPIO 0_C6 (BOM v1 line 60). It drives automatic backlight dimming.

## Application circuit and required passives

Application circuit (84366 p. 6, Fig. 8, and notes):

- C2 100 nF at VDD, close to the pin (the only decoupling normally needed).
- Optional R4 10 Ω series and C1 10 µF on VDD for a very disturbed supply.
- R1, R2 pull-ups on SDA, SCL: > 1 kΩ, for example 2.2 kΩ to 4.7 kΩ, to the 1.7–3.6 V bus rail.
- R3 pull-up on INT: 10 kΩ to 100 kΩ.
- ADDR tied to GND for address 0x10 (T1) or to VDD for 0x48.

## Interfaces and timing

- I2C standard and fast mode, 10 kHz to 400 kHz (p. 6). 7-bit address 0x10 (ADDR low) or 0x48 (ADDR high) (p. 6).
- 16-bit registers, LSB first: 00h ALS_CONF, 01h ALS_WH, 02h ALS_WL, 03h PSM, 04h ALS, 05h WHITE, 06h ALS_INT, 07h ID (p. 7). Register 00h defaults to shutdown (p. 7 note).
- ID register 07h: low byte 0x81; high byte 0xC4 for the 0x10 address option, 0xD4 for 0x48 (p. 9, Table 8).
- I2C timing, fast mode (p. 3): tBUF ≥ 1.3 µs, tHD;STA ≥ 0.6 µs, tSU;STA ≥ 0.6 µs, tSU;STO ≥ 0.6 µs, tHD;DAT 0–900 ns, tSU;DAT ≥ 100 ns, tLOW ≥ 1.3 µs, tHIGH ≥ 0.6 µs, tR and tF ≤ 300 ns. Standard mode has a clock/data low timeout of 25–35 ms (p. 3). Values are from the I2C specification and not tested in production (p. 3 note).

## Design notes for the T1

- VDD 2.5–3.6 V (p. 2): FP_3V3 is inside the range.
- The I2C3 bus runs at 3.3 V, within the 3.6 V maximum. INT is open-drain, so the BOM's 1.8 V pull-up to 0_C6 is compatible with the 3.3 V VDD (p. 6 notes). The 10–100 kΩ INT pull-up goes on the SoC side.
- The power-on default is shutdown (p. 7). The driver must clear ALS_SD before the first reading.
- Ambient operating range is −25 to +85 °C (p. 2), narrower than most other T1 parts.
- MSL 4, 72 h floor life (p. 1): note it in the assembly instructions.
- Optical aperture, cover-glass transmittance and lens design are faceplate topics. The 0.0042 lx/count resolution supports a dark lens (p. 1).
