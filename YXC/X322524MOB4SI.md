## Summary

The X322524MOB4SI is a 24 MHz AT-cut fundamental quartz crystal in a 3.2 × 2.5 mm four-pad ceramic package (YSX321SL, p. 1). In the T1 it is the 24 MHz reference of the RK3566 oscillator (BOM v1 line 15); the RK3566 expects a 24 MHz OSC input (RK3568 Datasheet Rev 1.0, p. 56, Table 3-2).

## Power by mode

Passive resonator. Drive power is set by the RK3566 oscillator and the load capacitors; it must stay within the drive-level limit above.

## Pinout and package

- Body 3.20 ± 0.1 × 2.50 ± 0.1 mm, height 0.7 ± 0.1 mm (YSX321SL, Dimensions; features list "3.20 × 2.50 × 0.70 mm").
- Four pads. The crystal connects #1 and #3; #2 and #4 are GND (YSX321SL, "Top View Crystal Connection").
- Pad #1 has a chamfered corner (YSX321SL, Bottom View).

## Recommended footprint / land pattern

YSX321SL, "Top View Suggested Layout": four pads 1.4 mm (horizontal) × 1.2 mm (vertical), 0.8 mm horizontal gap and 0.5 mm vertical gap between pads (overall 3.6 × 2.9 mm, derived).

## Application circuit and required passives

- One load capacitor from each crystal pin (#1, #3) to GND, sized from CL and the board stray capacitance. Values follow the RK3566 hardware design guide, not retrieved (**UNVERIFIED**, BOM v1 line 15).
- Pads #2 and #4 to GND.

## Interfaces and timing

24 MHz reference for the RK3566 PLLs (Frac PLL lock time 250 typ / 500 max input-clock cycles with FREF = 24 MHz; RK3568 Datasheet Rev 1.0, p. 58, Table 3-5).

## Design notes for the T1

- Confirm the load capacitance of the exact MPN with YXC or the distributor's parameter table before sizing the load capacitors; the series sheet does not give it.
- Short, symmetric traces from the RK3566 XIN/XOUT balls to pads #1/#3, with a ground reference under the crystal; no other signals under the part (general practice, **UNVERIFIED** against the RK3566 guide).
- The audio master clocks are separate oscillators (lines 20, 21); this crystal does not clock the DACs.
