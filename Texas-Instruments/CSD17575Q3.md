## Summary

A 30 V N-channel MOSFET with low RDS(on) and low gate charge in a 3.3 × 3.3 mm SON (SLPS489A, p. 1). TI fits it as the ship FET on the BQ25798 evaluation module (SLUUCB5E). In the T1 it sits between the BQ25798 BAT pins and the pack, driven by the charger's SDRV charge pump; it disconnects the pack in ship and shutdown modes so a stored T1 does not drain its battery, and it enables the charger's battery over-current protection (board ADR 0014 item 6).

## Application circuit and required passives

SLUSDV2C §7.3.12, Figure 7-15: the ship FET connects BAT (charger side) to the pack, gate on SDRV; BATP senses the pack side through 100 Ω. With the FET fitted, the charger's SFET_PRESENT bit is set (the mainline driver sets it) and no 1 nF SDRV-to-GND capacitor is needed (that capacitor is only for the unused case).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Drive:** SDRV is a 100 nA charge pump driving the gate to ≈ 5 V above VBAT (SLUSDV2C §7.3.12): VGS ≈ 5 V, 25 % of ±20 V, above the 1.8 V maximum threshold with RDS(on) specified at 4.5 V.
- **Margins (BRD-049):** VDS 30 V against the 2S pack (≤ 8.8 V): 29 %. 27 A continuous is 6.8× the 4 A of the brief and 8.4× the 3.2 A worst continuous case (≥ 2× required). The BQ25798 IBAT_OCP (9.3 A min) turns the FET off before its rating matters.
- **Leakage:** IGSS ≤ 100 nA against the 100 nA charge-pump current (**UNVERIFIED** that the gate reaches full drive at hot leakage; the EVM uses the same part).
