## Summary

The 744373240047 is a 0.47 µH ±20 % molded power inductor of the WE-LHMI series, size 4020: performance rated current 8.7 A (ΔT = 40 K), saturation current 10.4 A at a 10 % inductance drop and 19.6 A at 30 %, DC resistance 11.2 mΩ typ and 14 mΩ max at 20 °C (WE 003.000, p. 1). In the T1 it is the inductor of the TPS62826 3.3 V system buck (vcc3v3_sys), which BOM v1.3 moved off the DFE252012F-R47M because that part is rated below the TPS62826's maximum switch current limit and is not on TI's recommended list (BOM v1 lines 4, 6, 106; Changes in v1.3, item 6).

## Design notes for the T1

- **On TI's list:** BOM line 106 cites the TPS62826 datasheet table (SLVSEF9I Table 8-5) with 0.47 µH, 6.8 A and 11.2 mΩ max for this part. The Würth datasheet gives 11.2 mΩ as the *typical* RDC and 14 mΩ as the maximum (WE 003.000, p. 1). Use 14 mΩ for worst-case loss: 1.85² × 14 mΩ ≈ 48 mW at the 1.85 A estimated peak load (calculation, power tree v0 §3).
- **Saturation against the converter limit:** the TPS62826 5.0 A maximum switch limit (BOM line 6) is 48 % of ISAT,10% (10.4 A) and 57 % of IRP,40K (8.7 A). The figures on BOM line 106 match the datasheet.
- **Temperature:** ambient is rated to +85 °C only, against +125 °C operating temperature including self-heating (WE 003.000, p. 1). Check the internal case temperature near the buck.
- **Height:** 1.8 ± 0.2 mm, so 2.0 mm maximum (WE 003.000, p. 1). Height against the case is **UNVERIFIED** (BOM line 106).
- **Area:** 4.45 × 4.06 mm body, 5.2 × 2.4 mm land extent (WE 003.000, p. 1), against 2.5 × 2.0 mm for the DFE252012F-R47M.
- **Orientation:** the dot marks the winding start (WE 003.000, p. 1); the datasheet does not say which end should go to the SW node.
- **Audible noise:** Würth warns that audio-frequency currents may produce audible noise from the magnetostrictive material (WE 003.000, p. 7). It is relevant if the TPS62826 runs in power-save mode with bursts in the audio band.
- **Second source:** the Coilcraft [XFL4015-471MEC](../Coilcraft/XFL4015-471MEC.md) named on line 106 has a different recommended land (0.98 × 3.4 mm pads at 2.37 mm centre-to-centre, Coilcraft Doc 769-2) and a 3.5 A ISAT at 10 % drop. It is not a drop-in on this land pattern; a common land pattern is **UNVERIFIED**.
