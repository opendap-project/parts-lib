## Summary

NZ2520SDA crystal clock oscillator, specification number NSC5083D (+3.3 V ± 0.33 V, ±50 × 10⁻⁶, −40 to +85 °C) at 24.576 MHz (NZ2520SDA, p. 1–2). In the T1 it is the master clock for the 48 kHz sample-rate family, on the same MCLK net and the same 2520 land as the 22.5792 MHz part, fed through the passive attenuator into the CS43198 XTI/MCLK input; one oscillator runs at a time, the other in three-state standby (BOM v1 line 21; ADR 0010).

## Recommended operating conditions

NZ2520SDA, p. 1–2: supply +3.3 ± 0.33 V; −40 to +85 °C; ±50 × 10⁻⁶ overall tolerance; load ≤ 15 pF.

## Power by mode

NZ2520SDA, p. 1, max at +25 °C: operating 15 mA; standby 35 µA.

## Pinout and package

As NSC5083D-22.5792M: 2.5 mm × 2.0 mm × 0.9 mm max; #1 STAND-BY, #2 GND, #3 OUTPUT, #4 VCC (NZ2520SDA, p. 1).

## Recommended footprint

As NSC5083D-22.5792M: four 1.0 mm × 1.0 mm lands at 1.65 mm × 1.25 mm pitch (NZ2520SDA, p. 1). Pin 1 orientation between the dimension and land-pattern views to be confirmed (**UNVERIFIED**).

## Application circuit and required passives

About 0.01 µF bypass between VCC and GND close to the part; standby high or open = output on, low = high impedance (NZ2520SDA, p. 1).
