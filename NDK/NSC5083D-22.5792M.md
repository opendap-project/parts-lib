## Summary

The NZ2520SDA is a 2.5 mm × 2.0 mm CMOS-output crystal clock oscillator for 20–50 MHz, specified for low phase noise with a three-state standby input (NZ2520SDA, p. 1). NSC5083D is the specification number for the +3.3 V ± 0.33 V supply, ±50 × 10⁻⁶ overall tolerance and −40 to +85 °C (NZ2520SDA, p. 2); the order code adds the frequency, here 22.5792 MHz. In the T1 it is the master clock for the 44.1 kHz sample-rate family, shared on one MCLK net with the 24.576 MHz part and fed through a passive attenuator into the 1.8 V XTI/MCLK input of the CS43198 DACs; only one oscillator runs at a time (BOM v1 line 20; ADR 0010).

## Application circuit and required passives

- Bypass: approximately 0.01 µF between VCC and GND, close to the product (NZ2520SDA, p. 1).
- Output: CMOS, VOL ≤ 0.1 VCC, VOH ≥ 0.9 VCC, load ≤ 15 pF (NZ2520SDA, p. 1).
- Standby input #1: high (0.7 VCC ≤ VIH ≤ VCC) or open enables the output; low (VIL ≤ 0.3 VCC) sets the output to high impedance (NZ2520SDA, p. 1, Standby Function).

## Linux driver

None. The oscillator has no digital interface. In the device tree it can be described as a `fixed-clock` (or `gpio-gate-clock` with the standby GPIO) feeding the CS43198 MCLK; the binding choice is open (BOM v1 line 20: driver path *none*).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Supply:** dedicated MCLK_3V3 from a TPS7A2033 (ADR 0010; see [TPS7A2033PDBVR](../Texas-Instruments/TPS7A2033PDBVR.md)). NSC5083D requires 3.3 V ± 0.33 V (2.97–3.63 V).
- **Standby control at 3.3 V logic:** VIH ≥ 0.7 VCC means 1.8 V logic cannot enable the output reliably at 3.3 V supply; open = enabled. ADR 0010 drives ST from 3.3 V logic (TCA6416A or a single-bit translator, fixed in pinout v1). With both oscillators on one net, the default (open) state enables both outputs; the standby lines need a defined low on the inactive part from power-up.
- **Level into XTI:** the output swings to ≥ 0.9 VCC (≈ 3 V); the CS43198 XTI is in the 1.8 V VL domain. ADR 0010 uses a passive series-terminated attenuator; values and edge shape are pending SPICE with the CS43198 input capacitance. The 15 pF maximum load applies to the attenuator plus the net.
- **Tolerance:** ±50 × 10⁻⁶ overall; no tighter grade is listed for NZ2520SDA (p. 2).
- **Second source:** ADR 0010 names a Crystek CCHD-957-class 3.3 V XO, to be qualified; footprint compatibility **UNVERIFIED**.
