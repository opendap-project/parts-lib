## Summary

NZ2520SDA crystal clock oscillator, specification number NSC5083D (+3.3 V ± 0.33 V, ±50 × 10⁻⁶, −40 to +85 °C) at 45.1584 MHz (NZ2520SDA, pp. 1–2; the family covers 20–50 MHz). In the T1 it is the clock source for the 44.1 kHz sample-rate family: it shares one net with the other NSC5083D, only one runs at a time, and two SN74AUC1G74 flip-flops (one per CS43198) divide it by two, so the DACs receive an exact 50 % ± 1.7 % clock whatever this part's 40–60 % symmetry (board ADR 0019, which amends ADR 0010).

## Application circuit and required passives

About 0.01 µF bypass between VCC and GND close to the part; standby high or open = output on, low = high impedance (NZ2520SDA, p. 1). T1 (board ADR 0019): 33 Ω in series at the output; the shared net has a 100 kΩ pull-down at the dividers and carries at most 13.7 pF (two 2.5 pF divider inputs, ≤ 10 mm trace, pads, the other oscillator in standby), inside the 15 pF rating.

## Linux driver

None. The oscillator is not visible to the codec driver; the CS43198 sees the divided 22.5792 / 24.576 MHz clock (`fixed-clock`, or `gpio-gate-clock` with the standby line; board ADR 0019).

## Simulation models

None in the catalogue. Board `sim/mclk-divider` models the output as an ideal 1 ns edge behind 10, 30 or 181 Ω (181 Ω being the weakest linear driver that meets 6 ns into 15 pF).

## Design notes for the T1

- Supply MCLK_3V1 at 3.1 V (TPS7A2031), inside the 2.97–3.63 V range; the phase noise is catalogued at 3.3 V only (**UNVERIFIED** at 3.1 V).
- Standby at 3.3 V logic (VIH ≥ 0.7 VCC; open = on), pulled down so that both oscillators are off from power-up, as in ADR 0010. Keep it low unless MCLK_1V8 (the dividers' supply) is up.
- **Frequency:** Digi-Key lists the part as "45.158 MHz"; a third-party listing names it NZ2520SDA 45.1584M-NSC5083D. Confirm 45.1584 MHz on an NDK document before ordering (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Stock:** LCSC has none; Digi-Key is the source. Request the 45.1584 MHz phase noise from NDK, or measure it on the first boards.
