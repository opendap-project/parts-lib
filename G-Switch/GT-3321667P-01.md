## Summary

A 4.4 mm, 5-pole phone jack for surface mounting, with a detect switch, gold-flashed contacts and two locating pegs (drawing C-GT03321667P-01). In the T1 it is the balanced headphone output (L+, L−, R+, R−, GND) fed by two OPA1622 packages; it is the stocked primary, with a genuine Pentaconn part as a quoted upgrade on a shared land if the drawings match (BOM v1 line 31, T1-029).

## Design notes for the T1

- The jack carries both halves of each balanced channel on paired pins (R− on 2 and 3, R+ on 4 and 5, L+ on 7 and 8); tie each pair together on the board. L− appears only on pin 6 in the drawing schematic: confirm against a sample, **UNVERIFIED**.
- Sharing a land with a genuine Pentaconn jack needs the Pentaconn drawing, which is not public (see `Nippon-DICS/Pentaconn-4.4-mm-jack.md`).
- Housing plastic is rated UL94V-HB, not V-0 (drawing BOM, items 13–14).
- Reflow compatibility is not stated on the drawing: **UNVERIFIED**; confirm with the supplier before assembly.
