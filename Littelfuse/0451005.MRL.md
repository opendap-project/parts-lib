## Summary

The 451/453 Series is a very small wire-in-air, square surface-mount fuse for secondary-side over-current protection, very fast-acting, rated 0.062 A to 20 A (451/453 DS, p. 1). The part number decodes as series 0451 (gold-plated caps, RoHS and halogen free with the "L" suffix), amp code 005., quantity code M (1,000 per reel), packaging code R (tape and reel) (p. 4, Part Numbering System). In the T1 it protects the two-cell battery path, sized by board ADR 0014 for the worst continuous case of ≈ 3.2 A at VSYS 6.6 V (full HAT and USB-C budget, full-scale balanced headphones and a 3 W system at once): 64 % of 5 A.

## Recommended operating conditions

- Opening time (p. 1): at 100 % of rating, 4 hours minimum; at 200 %, 5 s maximum (0.062–10 A).
- Continuous operation: 25 % standard derating, plus the temperature re-rating curve (p. 3): about 96–97 % at 60 °C ambient (approximate, read from the plot in the [045102.5MRL](045102.5MRL.md) sheet).
- Average time-current curves on p. 3 (graph only).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Margin (BRD-049):** 3.2 A / 5 A = 64 % (2.4 A at the full budget without the headphone term = 48 %), inside the 75 % fuse rule with room for the temperature re-rating.
- **What it bounds:** opening is guaranteed only at 200 % (10 A within 5 s, p. 1). It is the short-circuit protection; a sustained overload is bounded by the pack PCM (trip 4.0–5.0 A, board ADR 0014 item 2) and the BQ25798 IBAT_OCP (9.3 A minimum).
- **Interrupting rating:** a 2S pack's prospective short-circuit current is **UNVERIFIED**; the relevant DC figure is 300 A at 32 V DC.
