## Summary

The 451/453 Series is a very small wire-in-air, square surface-mount fuse for secondary-side over-current protection, very fast-acting, rated 0.062 A to 20 A (451/453 DS, p. 1). The part number decodes as series 0451 (gold-plated caps, RoHS and halogen free with the "L" suffix), amp code 02.5 or 007, quantity code M (1,000 per reel), packaging code R (tape and reel) (p. 4, Part Numbering System). In the T1 it protects the battery lead and connector against a sustained overload that neither the pack PCM (trip not published for the Adafruit 328) nor the BQ25895 BATFET OCP (9 A) bounds (BOM v1 line 104, v1.2 changes 4 and 5; BRD-049).

## Recommended operating conditions

- Opening time (p. 1, Electrical Characteristics for Series): at 100 % of the ampere rating, 4 hours minimum (0.062–20 A); at 200 %, 5 s maximum (0.062–10 A).
- Continuous operation: "standard derating of 25 %", to which the temperature re-rating curve is added (p. 3, Temperature Re-rating Curve, note 1).
- Temperature re-rating curve (p. 3): 100 % at 25 °C, falling roughly linearly to about 91 % at 120 °C and rising to about 109 % at −60 °C (values read from the plot, approximate).
- Average time-current curves for every rating are on p. 3 (graph only; no tabulated points).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Derating rule.** The BOM rule "fuses ≤ 75 %" (power tree v0 §4.10) matches the datasheet's 25 % standard derating for continuous operation (451/453 DS, p. 3, note 1). The temperature re-rating is in addition: at an ambient of 60 °C the curve reads about 96–97 %, so the continuous limit becomes about 72–73 % (approximate, read from the plot). Interim 1.6 A / 2.5 A = 64 % and full budget 4.8 A / 7 A = 69 % both stay under it; the 7 A part keeps only a few percent of margin above about 60 °C ambient near the fuse.
- **What the interim fuse bounds.** At 100 % of rating the fuse is guaranteed not to open for 4 hours; only at 200 % (5 A for the 2.5 A part) is opening guaranteed within 5 s (p. 1). It therefore does not open on a sustained 2.0–2.5 A overload of the PH contact rated 2 A with AWG #24 (power tree v0 §4.10); it bounds a short or a heavy fault. The time to open between 100 % and 200 % comes only from the p. 3 curves.
- **Interrupting rating.** The relevant DC figure for a 1S cell is 300 A at 32 V DC (2.5 A, 3 A) or 400 A at 32 V DC (7 A) (p. 2). The prospective short-circuit current of the pack is **UNVERIFIED**; the BOM line quoted only 50 A at 125 V from the LCSC listing.
- **Footprint compatibility.** 2.5 A, 3 A and 7 A share the same series drawing and pad layout (p. 4), so the 7 A part drops onto the interim footprint (as power tree v0 states for the full-budget chain).
- Agency approvals differ per rating (p. 2 table); the T1 relies on none of them.
