## Summary

The 451/453 Series is a very small wire-in-air, square surface-mount fuse for secondary-side over-current protection, very fast-acting, rated 0.062 A to 20 A (451/453 DS, p. 1). The part number decodes as series 0451 (gold-plated caps, RoHS and halogen free with the "L" suffix), amp code 004., quantity code M (1,000 per reel), packaging code R (tape and reel) (p. 4, Part Numbering System). In the T1 it protects the two-cell battery path (board ADR 0014 item 1): 2.4 A at the full output budget is 60 % of 4 A.

## Design notes for the T1

- **Margin (BRD-049):** 2.4 A / 4 A = 60 %, inside the 75 % fuse rule (power tree v0 §4.10), with room for the temperature re-rating.
- **What it bounds:** opening is guaranteed only at 200 % (8 A within 5 s, p. 1). It is the short-circuit protection; a sustained overload between 4 and 8 A is bounded by the pack PCM (trip 3.0–4.0 A, board ADR 0014 item 2). The BQ25798 IBAT_OCP is 9.3 A minimum.
- **Interrupting rating:** a 2S pack's prospective short-circuit current is **UNVERIFIED**; the relevant DC figure is 300 A at 32 V DC.
- **Open question (board BOM §7 item 17):** with full-scale headphones on top of the full HAT and USB-C budget the battery current reaches ≈ 3.2 A, 79 % of this fuse; a 5 A part (0451005.MRL, same footprint per the series table) would be the step if the maintainers grow the budget.
