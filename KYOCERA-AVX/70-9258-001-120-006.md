## Summary

A single gold-plated copper-alloy spring finger, soldered to the board, that presses on a flat gold pad of the mating part. Rated 6 A, 600 V AC (TDS-BTB-0026). Its type-120 deflected-height range, 1.6–2.8 mm, contains the T1's board-to-pack working height (2.1 ± 0.3 mm, board ADR 0020). In the T1, six of them connect the battery pack through pads on the pack, in a window of the pocket floor.

## Design notes for the T1

- **Current per pole** (board ADR 0020): two contacts in parallel per power pole. With a sharing factor of 1.25 (assumption until measured), the worst continuous 3.2 A puts 2.0 A on the hotter contact, 33 % of 6 A; the 5 A PCM and fuse limit puts 3.1 A, 52 %. Pole rating counted at 0.8 × 2 × 6 A = 9.6 A, against the ADR 0014 criterion of ≥ 4.6 A per pole.
- **Working height:** board top to pack pad 2.1 mm nominal (1.5 mm component zone + 0.4 mm pocket floor + 0.2 mm pad recess); ± 0.3 mm tolerance (assumption until the case tolerance stack exists) gives 1.8–2.4 mm, 0.2 mm and 0.4 mm inside the 1.6–2.8 mm range. A pack lifted by 0.5 mm in a drop still sits inside the range.
- **Cycles:** 2,500 rated, against at most tens of pack replacements over a T1's life plus factory and service handling.
- **Force:** unknown until 201-01-252 is obtained or the contact is measured (qualification CB-01). The back cover's foam preload must exceed six times the contact force at 2.1 mm with margin (case input).
- **Request from KYOCERA AVX before the board freezes:** product specification 201-01-252 (contact force, contact resistance initial and after durability and environment, current-rating basis) and application note 201-01-251.
- **No second source on the same land.** Studied alternatives: Mill-Max 0990-0-15-20-76-14-11-0 pogo pins (2 A, 1.50–2.49 mm working height; four per pole needed) and AVX 00-9155-002-101-006 (3 A per way, 1.30–1.95 mm operating height, below the T1 working height with the recess): [T1 research](https://github.com/opendap-project/board/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-contact-battery-t1.md) §3.
