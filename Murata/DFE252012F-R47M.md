## Summary

The DFE252012F-R47M is a 0.47 µH ±20 % metal-alloy, magnetically shielded power inductor in a 2.5 × 2.0 × 1.2 mm (max) body (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, pp. 1–2). In the T1 it is the inductor of the vdd_cpu buck (BOM v1 line 4) and of the five RK809-5 buck converters BUCK1–BUCK5 (line 5), six pieces in total.

## Power by mode

Passive part. Copper loss is I² × DCR: at the 4 A BUCK1/2/5 rating quoted in the BOM, 4² × 0.023 Ω = 0.37 W at maximum DCR (derived from the p. 2 DCR). Core loss is not specified in the document.

## Pinout and package

- Two terminals, no polarity, no marking on the body (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 1).
- Body 2.5 ± 0.2 × 2.0 ± 0.2 mm, height 1.2 mm max; terminal length 0.6 ± 0.2 mm (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 1).
- Mass 0.0331 g typical (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 1).
- Packing: 8 mm tape, 4 mm pitch, 3,000 pieces per ϕ180 mm reel (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 6).

## Recommended footprint / land pattern

Recommended PCB pattern (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 5): two pads 2.0 mm long (along the 2.0 mm body side), 2.8 mm overall outer span, 1.2 mm gap between pads, so each pad is 0.8 mm wide (derived: (2.8 − 1.2) / 2).

The core has low insulation resistance: no vias and no copper under the coil other than the terminal pads, and no other part touching the body (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 7, notice 2).

## Design notes for the T1

- The BOM quotes RK809 BUCK1, 2 and 5 at 4 A and BUCK3, 4 at 3 A (line 5). Against the 4.9 A rated current (p. 2) the margin at 4 A DC is 0.9 A; the peak current (DC + half the ripple) must stay below the rating. The ripple depends on the RK809 switching frequency, which is not in this document.
- The rated current based on temperature rise is measured on Murata's 6-layer test board (p. 2); on the T1 board the real temperature rise must be checked in the product (p. 7, notice 4).
- The vdd_cpu buck current (line 4) is **UNVERIFIED** until the RK860-0 data is available (BOM v1 line 4).
- Keep the keep-out under the body (p. 7) in the footprint drawn in `kicad-lib`.
