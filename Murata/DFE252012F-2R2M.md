## Summary

The DFE252012F-2R2M is a 2.2 µH ±20 % metal-alloy, magnetically shielded power inductor in a 2.5 × 2.0 × 1.2 mm (max) body (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, pp. 1–2). In the T1 it is the inductor of the TLV62569 3.3 V system buck (BOM v1 line 6, via line 7) and of the buck on line 38 (Power subsystem).

## Power by mode

Passive part. Copper loss is I² × DCR: at 2 A, 2² × 0.082 Ω = 0.33 W at maximum DCR (derived from the p. 2 DCR). Core loss is not specified in the document.

## Recommended footprint

Recommended PCB pattern (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 5; same on the DFE252012F catalogue): two pads 2.0 mm long, 2.8 mm overall outer span, 1.2 mm gap, so each pad is 0.8 mm wide (derived: (2.8 − 1.2) / 2).

No vias and no copper under the coil other than the terminal pads, and no other part touching the body (J(E)TE243A-0017B-01, p. 7, notice 2).

## Design notes for the T1

- The TLV62569 on line 6 is a 2 A buck (BOM v1 line 6). The rated current of this inductor is 2.3 A (p. 2), set by temperature rise, not saturation (3.3 A). At a 2 A load the peak current (2 A + half the ripple) must stay below 2.3 A for the temperature-rise rating, and well below 3.3 A for saturation; check the ripple with the TLV62569 switching frequency and the actual vcc3v3_sys load.
- The inductor value must fall inside the TLV62569 recommended range; that check belongs to the TLV62569 sheet.
- Keep the keep-out under the body (p. 7) in the footprint.
