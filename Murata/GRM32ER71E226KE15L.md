## Summary

A 22 µF, 25 V, X7R ceramic capacitor in 1210, decoded from the Murata part number (GRM series, 32 = 1210, E = 2.5 mm thickness code, R7 = X7R, 1E = 25 V, 226 = 22 µF, K = ±10 %; decoding **UNVERIFIED** against Murata's numbering guide).
