## Summary

A 4.7 µH ±20 % metal-alloy, magnetically shielded power inductor with flat-wire winding (DFE252012F sheet, features and table). In the T1 two parts are the inductors of the TPS65131 ±6 V headphone-amplifier pre-regulator (BOM v1 line 23). The TPS65131 datasheet example uses 4.7 µH (BOM note). The orderable Murata number is DFE252012F-4R7M=P2.

## Design notes for the T1

- DCR 190 mΩ max is high for a power path; at 0.5 A RMS it dissipates about 48 mW per inductor. If efficiency at the headphone load matters, compare the DFE252012F-3R3M (135 mΩ) only if the TPS65131 compensation allows 3.3 µH (**UNVERIFIED**).
- Shielded construction limits coupling into the audio path, but keep the switch nodes away from the DAC and OPA1622 inputs.
