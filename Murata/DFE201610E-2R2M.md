- **Package:** 2016 (2.0 × 1.6 mm), 1.0 mm max height

## Summary

A 2.2 µH ±20 % metal-alloy power inductor in a 2.0 × 1.6 mm case (JTE243A-0001 pp. 1–2). In the T1 it is the inductor of the TPS62840 2.2 V audio pre-buck that feeds the DAC VA LDOs (BOM v1 line 28). The orderable Murata number is DFE201610E-2R2M=P2. The BOM lists DFE201610E-1R0M as an alternative only if the TPS62840 datasheet allows 1 µH.

## Power by mode

Not applicable (passive). Copper loss ≈ IRMS² × 140 mΩ.

## Pinout and package

Two terminals, no polarity. Body 2.0 ± 0.2 × 1.6 ± 0.2 mm, height 1.0 mm max, terminal length 0.5 ± 0.3 mm (JTE243A-0001 p. 1). Halogen-free, RoHS (p. 1). 3000 pcs per 180 mm reel, 8 mm tape (p. 6).

## Recommended footprint / land pattern

JTE243A-0001 p. 5, "Recommended PCB pattern": two pads, overall 2.4 mm, gap 0.8 mm (so each pad 0.8 mm long), pad width 1.8 mm.

## Application circuit and required passives

Switch-node inductor of the TPS62840 (see the TPS62840DLCR sheet). The audio pre-buck load is small (two DAC VA LDOs), far below the 1.7 A rating.

## Design notes for the T1

- The core has low insulation resistance: no vias or copper under the body except the pads, and no component touching the inductor (JTE243A-0001 p. 7, precaution 2).
- Murata recommends infrared reflow, not halogen-lamp heating (p. 5).
