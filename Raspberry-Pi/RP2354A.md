## Summary

The RP2354A is the RP2350 microcontroller (dual-core, 30 user GPIOs in the QFN-60 package) with 2 MB of QSPI flash stacked in the package (DS Table 1, p. 13; §14.3, p. 1327). The in-package flash is a Winbond W25Q16JVWI on a separate die, connected to the same six QSPI pads that are also bonded out (DS §14.3, p. 1327). In other respects, including the pinout, it is identical to the RP2350A (DS §14.3, p. 1327). In the T1 it is the helper coprocessor (BOM v1 line 12, board ADR 0004); BRD-005 keeps it out of every data path. The BOM plans IOVDD at 1.8 V so the RP2354A can tap the 1.8 V I2S lines without translators.

## Application circuit and required passives

Core regulator (DS §6.3.7–6.3.8, pp. 407–413; HDG §2.1, pp. 5–7):

| Ref | Part | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| L (VREG_LX) | Abracon AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T (BOM line 13) | Fully shielded, 3.3 µH ±20 %, DCR ≤ 250 mΩ, Isat ≥ 1.5 A, polarity-marked, orientation as in DS Figure 26 (DS §6.3.8.2, p. 412) |
| CIN (VREG_VIN) | 4.7 µF, 0402 | ≥ 4.7 µF, ESR ≤ 50 mΩ, own GND via to the QFN pad |
| COUT (DVDD) | 4.7 µF, 0402 | 4.7 µF ±20 %, ESR ≤ 250 mΩ, ESL ≤ 6 nH; a second 4.7 µF at DVDD pin 23 is recommended |
| VREG_AVDD filter | 33 Ω + 4.7 µF | RC low-pass; VREG_AVDD draws about 200 µA (HDG p. 7) |

Decoupling (DS §6.1, pp. 400–401; HDG §2.2.1, p. 8): 100 nF per power pin; with the internal regulator, 100 nF on the two DVDD pins closest to the regulator and 4.7 µF on the furthest (pin 23); 100 nF on USB_OTP_VDD; 100 nF on ADC_AVDD; 4.7 µF on VREG_VIN. The minimal design shares one capacitor between pins 53 and 54 for space (HDG p. 8).

Clock (BOM v1.1 line 14): an ECS-2016MV-120-BN-TR 1.6–3.6 V 12 MHz oscillator on RP_IOVDD_1V8 drives XIN, XOUT open; XIN accepts a 1–50 MHz square wave (DS Table 1686, p. 1338). See [`ECS/ECS-2016MV-120-BN-TR.md`](../ECS/ECS-2016MV-120-BN-TR.md). The crystal circuit below is the BOM's second source only. Crystal (HDG §4, pp. 13–14): Abracon ABM8-272-T3 with two 15 pF load capacitors to ground and a 1 kΩ series resistor on XOUT; the circuit is tuned for IOVDD = 3.3 V. With IOVDD below 3.3 V the XOSC drive is lower and the crystal amplitude drops or it may not oscillate; a smaller series resistor is then needed and the circuit must be re-tuned and tested over temperature (HDG p. 13–14).

Boot: on RP2354, keep a BOOTSEL path on QSPI_SS (1 kΩ to a switch to GND in the minimal design); the external flash footprint is not needed (HDG §3.1, pp. 10–11). USB requires 27 Ω series resistors on DP and DM (DS Table 1678, p. 1334).

## Interfaces and timing

- GPIO electrical levels at IOVDD = 1.8 V (DS Table 1683, p. 1336): VIH min 0.65 × IOVDD, VIL max 0.35 × IOVDD, VOH min 1.24 V, VOL max 0.3 V; input leakage ≤ 1 µA; pull-up 32–106 kΩ, pull-down 35–189 kΩ (p. 1337). Total IOVDD source current ≤ 100 mA, QSPI ≤ 20 mA (p. 1337).
- At IOVDD = 1.8 V the pad VOLTAGE_SELECT register must be set to 1; leaving it at 0 is safe but thresholds are out of specification, and setting 1 with IOVDD above 1.8 V can damage the chip (DS §6.1.1, p. 400).
- FT pins tolerate up to 3.63 V when IOVDD = 1.8 V (DS Table 1680, p. 1335).
- System clock to GPIO output delay max 4.9 ns at IOVDD = 1.8 V, 3.5 ns at 3.3 V (QFN-60) (DS Table 1401, p. 1143).
- SWD: SWCLK up to 50 MHz; host-to-target data centre-aligned with SWCLK (DS Table 1687, p. 1339).
- ADC: 12 bits resolved, ENOB 9 min / 9.5 typ, input range 0 to ADC_AVDD, but ADC inputs must not exceed IOVDD (DS Table 1685, p. 1338; §6.1.5, p. 401).
- USB 1.1 PHY on USB_DP/USB_DM, powered from USB_OTP_VDD (DS Table 1678, p. 1334).

## Design notes for the T1

- QSPI_IOVDD (pin 54) must be 3.3 V on the RP2354A (DS Table 1688 note, p. 1341), so the part needs a 3.3 V rail even with IOVDD at 1.8 V. USB_OTP_VDD and VREG_AVDD are also 3.135–3.63 V supplies.
- IOVDD at 1.8 V (BOM line 12) affects the crystal: the ABM8-272-T3 circuit is only tuned for 3.3 V IOVDD (HDG §4.1, p. 14). Either re-tune the series resistor and qualify start-up over temperature, or drive XIN with a 1.8 V square wave (DS Table 1686, p. 1338). BOM v1.1 (D6) takes the second path: the ECS-2016MV-120-BN-TR oscillator on RP_IOVDD_1V8; the ABM8-272-T3 stays a second source after re-tuning and qualification.
- Firmware must set VOLTAGE_SELECT = 1 for bank 0 at boot when IOVDD = 1.8 V.
- Follow the Pico 2 regulator layout (DS Figure 26) closely: place the regulator first, keep CIN/LX/COUT on one side, cut copper under LX on layer 2 (DS §6.3.8.1, pp. 411–412).
- VREG_VIN may come from a rail other than 3.3 V (2.7–5.5 V), but VREG_VIN and VREG_AVDD must ramp together (DS §6.1.8, p. 401).
