## Summary

A shielded 3.3 µH molded power inductor with a polarity mark, made by Abracon for the RP235x internal core regulator and supplied on reels with all parts oriented the same way (Rev A p. 1; RP2350 Datasheet §6.3.8.3, p. 413). In the T1 it is the VREG_LX inductor of the RP2354A coprocessor (BOM v1 line 13). Raspberry Pi names no second source.

## Design notes for the T1

- Orientation is a functional requirement, not cosmetic: a reversed inductor degrades the RP2350 regulator at higher currents and load transients (RP2350 Datasheet §6.3.8.3, p. 413; Hardware design with RP2350 §2.1, p. 6). Put the polarity mark on the silkscreen and in the assembly drawing.
- Cut copper beneath the inductor on the top layer and beneath the LX node on layer 2 (RP2350 Datasheet §6.3.8.1, pp. 411–412).
