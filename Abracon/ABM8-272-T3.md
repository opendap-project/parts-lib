- **Package:** 3.2 × 2.5 mm ceramic, 4 pads

## Summary

A 12.000 MHz fundamental AT-cut crystal with 10 pF load capacitance (#456603 p. 1). Raspberry Pi recommends it for RP2350 designs and tuned the Pico 2 oscillator circuit for it (Hardware design with RP2350 §4–4.1, pp. 13–14). In the T1 it clocks the RP2354A coprocessor's XOSC (BOM v1 line 14).

## Power by mode

Not applicable (passive resonator). Drive level is set by the RP2354A XOSC and the series resistor; keep it within 200 µW (#456603 p. 1).

## Pinout and package

Drawing #456603, p. 2, "Mechanical Dimensions". Body 3.2 ± 0.2 × 2.5 ± 0.2 mm, height 0.6 ± 0.15 mm. Pins 1 and 3 are the crystal terminals and pins 2 and 4 are not connected to the crystal, as drawn in the internal-connection sketch (#456603 p. 2; pin function **UNVERIFIED** as text, read from the drawing). The chamfer may be on pin 1, 3 or 4 and has no electrical significance (#456603 p. 2). MSL 1, hermetically sealed (#456603 p. 1). 3000 pcs per reel (#456603 p. 4).

## Recommended footprint / land pattern

Drawing #456603, p. 2: four pads 1.4 mm wide × 1.1 mm high, horizontal gap 0.8 mm between pad columns, vertical centre spacing 1.6 mm. Reflow profile on #456603 p. 3 (graphic only; values not transcribed).

## Application circuit and required passives

Raspberry Pi minimal design (Hardware design with RP2350 §4, p. 13): crystal between XIN and XOUT, 15 pF from each terminal to GND (series 7.5 pF plus about 3 pF stray gives ≈ 10.5 pF against the 10 pF CL), and 1 kΩ series resistor on XOUT to limit drive. This combination is tuned for IOVDD = 3.3 V.

## Interfaces and timing

Not applicable. RP2350 XOSC accepts 1–50 MHz; USB boot by default needs a 12 MHz reference (RP2350 Datasheet Table 1686, p. 1338).

## Design notes for the T1

- The T1 plans RP2354A IOVDD at 1.8 V (BOM line 12). The crystal oscillator runs from IOVDD; at less than 3.3 V the drive and amplitude are lower and the crystal may not start. Raspberry Pi says a smaller series resistor is then needed and the circuit must be re-tuned and tested over temperature (Hardware design with RP2350 §4–4.1, pp. 13–14). This is an open design item for line 14.
- Keep XIN/XOUT traces short; stray capacitance is part of the load (Hardware design with RP2350 p. 13).
