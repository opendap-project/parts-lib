# FH35C-51S-0.3SHW

- **Manufacturer:** Hirose Electric
- **Function:** 51-contact FPC connector, 0.3 mm pitch, 0.9 mm height, back-flip actuator, dual-sided (top and bottom) contacts
- **BOM v1 lines:** 71 (board-side faceplate connector), ordered as FH35C-51S-0.3SHW(50), quantity 1
- **Package:** SMT, horizontal staggered contacts, 16.9 × 3.2 mm (connector width A and depth)
- **Datasheet:** FH35C Series catalog, edition 2013.11, copyright stamp Aug. 1, 2019, 14 pages (copy obtained from the LCSC datasheet mirror, part C424665)
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The FH35C is a 0.3 mm-pitch, 0.9 mm-high FPC connector that contacts both sides of a 0.2 mm FPC, with a back-flip lock and a stated MIPI D-PHY and eDP 1.3 compatibility (FH35C catalog, p. 1, features 1–3, 6). In the T1 the 51-contact version is the board-side faceplate connector (BOM v1 line 71): DSI four lanes, I2C, SPI/DBI, INT/RST, TE, status LED, PDM, detect, backlight anode and cathode, and the FP_3V3, FP_1V8, FP_VCI and MIC_1V8 supplies, with 44 contacts used and 7 spare.

## Pinout and package

Part number (p. 2): FH35C (dual-sided, halogen-free) – 51S (51 contacts) – 0.3 (pitch) SHW (SMT horizontal staggered array) (50) (standard, 5,000 per reel). (99) is 500 per reel. HRS No. CL580-2920-9-50 (p. 3).

Connector dimensions for 51 contacts (p. 3, Connector Dimension Table): A = 16.9 ± 0.15, B = 14.4 ± 0.1, C = 15 ± 0.1, D = 15.63 (reference), E = 16.33 ± 0.1 mm. Depth 3 mm (body) and 3.2 mm with the actuator, height 0.9 ± 0.1 mm; actuator opens to 90° and reaches 1.8 mm (reference) (p. 3, detail a; p. 1). Contact coplanarity 0.1 mm maximum (p. 3, note 1).

Contacts sit in two staggered rows at 0.6 mm pitch per row: (n−1)/2 = 25 contacts in one row and (n+1)/2 = 26 in the other (p. 4). Contact 1 position and the top/bottom assignment of odd and even contacts are on the drawing for approval, not in this catalog: **UNVERIFIED**.

## Recommended footprint

Recommended land dimensions, n = 51 (p. 4):

- Row with (n−1)/2 lands: 0.3 ± 0.02 mm wide on 0.6 mm pitch, 0.65 ± 0.03 mm long; span B = 14.4 mm.
- Row with (n+1)/2 lands: 0.3 ± 0.02 mm wide on 0.6 mm pitch, 0.8 ± 0.03 mm long; span C = 15 mm.
- Rows offset 0.3 mm (stagger); 2.15 ± 0.03 mm between the rows; overall 3.6 mm (reference).
- Metal fittings: 0.4 ± 0.03 × 0.95 ± 0.03 mm lands, 0.5 ± 0.03 and 0.2 ± 0.03 mm locating dimensions, positional tolerance 0.02 mm to datum X.

Recommended metal mask (t = 0.1 mm) (p. 4): openings 0.23 ± 0.02 mm wide; 0.55 ± 0.02 mm (short row) and 0.65 ± 0.02 mm (long row); 2.4 ± 0.02 mm between rows; fitting openings 0.3 ± 0.02 × 0.7 ± 0.02 mm (0.9 ± 0.02 and 0.2 ± 0.02 mm locating).

## Design notes for the T1

- 0.2 A per contact, derated to 70 % (0.14 A) when all contacts carry current (p. 2, Note 1). The backlight string runs at 60 mA (display spec, line 81) and fits one contact per polarity; FP_3V3 is already allocated two contacts (line 71). Every supply contact count must be checked against 0.14 A per contact.
- 30 V AC rms voltage rating (p. 2) is above the 16.5 V maximum LED-string forward voltage (line 81), which answers the BOM's "16.5 V LED-string rating UNVERIFIED" for voltage; the catalog states the rating in AC rms only.
- Rated for 10 mating cycles (p. 2): the faceplate is service-replaceable, not user-swappable, at this connector.
- FPC tail: 0.2 ± 0.03 mm thick at the mating area (p. 2); recommended FPC outline F = 15.6 ± 0.05 mm for 51 contacts with a stiffener film (p. 5); N ≥ 0.5 mm (p. 5, note 6). On a double-sided FPC, remove the back-side copper in the mating area to avoid unintended unlocking (p. 6).
- Use differential pairs on identical contact rows for DSI (p. 1, feature 3).
- The bottom of the connector is over-moulded, so traces may run under it (p. 1, feature 7).
