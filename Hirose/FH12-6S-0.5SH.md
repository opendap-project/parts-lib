# FH12-6S-0.5SH

- **Manufacturer:** Hirose Electric
- **Function:** 6-contact FPC/FFC connector, 0.5 mm pitch, bottom contact, flip-lock actuator, 2 mm height
- **BOM v1 lines:** 85 (touch-panel tail on the touch-faceplate flex), ordered as FH12-6S-0.5SH(55), quantity 1
- **Package:** SMT horizontal (SH), 7.1 × 5.6 mm overall (width C × depth), 2 mm high
- **Datasheet:** FH12 Series catalog, edition Jan. 1, 2021 (20 pages); copy obtained from the LCSC datasheet mirror (part C596730). The copy in the FH12-6S cache folder (part C202118) is an older edition, Nov. 1, 2016, not used here.
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The FH12 is a 0.5 mm-pitch FPC/FFC connector with a flip-lock actuator; the FH12-**S-0.5SH is the bottom-contact, SMT horizontal version, available from 6 to 53 contacts (FH12 catalog 2021, pp. 3–4). In the T1 the 6-contact version mates the capacitive touch panel (CTP) tail on the touch faceplate flex (BOM v1 line 85); the display specification names this connector for that tail. The (55) suffix is the gold-plated version (p. 2, item 10).

## Pinout and package

HRS No. 586-0582-5 followed by the plating code (p. 4). Dimensions for 6 contacts (p. 4 table): A = 2.5 mm (first to last contact), B = 6.1 mm, C = 7.1 mm (overall width), D = 3.57 mm. Depth 4.6 mm (body) and 5.6 mm overall; height 2 mm (1.85 mm reference to the actuator); contacts protrude 0.8 mm; terminal coplanarity 0.1 mm maximum (p. 4, drawing and note 1). The body carries a polarizing mark and a number-of-contacts indicator (p. 4). Contacts are bottom contacts: the FPC conductors face the PCB (p. 3, series configuration). Packaging: embossed tape, 2,000 or 500 pieces per reel (p. 4, table note 1).

## Recommended footprint

0.5 mm pitch bottom-contact type, n = 6 (p. 10, Land / Metal Mask dimensions):

- Signal lands 0.3 ± 0.03 mm wide on 0.5 ± 0.05 mm pitch, 1.3 ± 0.1 mm long; span of the first to last land centres A = 2.5 ± 0.05 mm.
- Two metal-fitting lands, 2.2 ± 0.1 mm long; outer span B = 8.1 ± 0.1 mm, inner span C = 4.5 ± 0.1 mm (so each fitting land is 1.8 mm wide).
- Metal mask openings 0.25 ± 0.03 mm wide for the signal lands; recommended mask thickness 0.15 mm.

## Design notes for the T1

- Mating FPC (the display/touch tail, fixed by the module vendor): 0.3 ± 0.05 mm at the contact area (p. 2), conductors 0.35 ± 0.03 mm wide on 0.5 mm pitch, stiffener 0.188 mm (7.5 mil) minimum, FPC width B = 3.5 ± 0.07 mm for 6 contacts (p. 10, FPC/FFC dimensions). The module's tail drawing must be checked against these values.
- 0.5 A per contact, 0.35 A when all contacts carry current (p. 2, Note 1).
- Rated for 20 insertion/withdrawal cycles (p. 2): an assembly connector on the faceplate.
- Contact 1 location relative to the polarizing mark: check against the Hirose drawing for approval (**UNVERIFIED** from the catalog).
