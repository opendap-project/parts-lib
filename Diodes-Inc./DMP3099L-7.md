## Summary

A small −30 V P-channel MOSFET with low gate threshold (DS36081, p. 1). In the T1 it is the high-side switch of the SNVA856B Figure 7-1 (right) level shifter that drives the LMR33630C EN pin, whose ground is the −7 V rail: with its source on VSYS, it feeds a 100 kΩ / 100 kΩ divider to −7 V whose midpoint is EN (board BOM line 119).

## Absolute maximum ratings

DS36081, p. 2 (TA 25 °C): VDSS −30 V; VGSS ±20 V; ID −3.8 A (TA 25 °C) and −2.9 A (70 °C) at VGS −10 V; IDM −11 A; PD 1.08 W; RθJA 115 °C/W; TJ, Tstg −55 to 150 °C.

## Application circuit and required passives

SNVA856B Figure 7-1 (right): `HPA_RAIL_EN` (3.3 V logic) drives a DMG1012T N-FET; its drain pulls the DMP3099L gate, held at VSYS by 100 kΩ, low; the DMP3099L drives the EN divider (100 kΩ / 100 kΩ to −7 V).

## Interfaces and timing
