# DMG1012T-7

- **Manufacturer:** Diodes Incorporated
- **Function:** 20 V N-channel enhancement-mode MOSFET with ESD gate protection
- **BOM v1 lines:** 35 (Audio, mic power switch and privacy-LED gate); also used in the MaskROM circuit (line 97)
- **Package:** SOT523, 3 leads
- **Datasheet:** Diodes Incorporated DMG1012T, document DS31783 Rev. 8 - 2, February 2022, 7 pages (cited as "DS31783"); sha256 in `manifest.toml`
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

An N-channel enhancement-mode MOSFET, 20 V, with VGS(th) 0.5–1.0 V and RDS(on) specified down to VGS = 1.8 V, and an ESD gate-protection diode rated to 2 kV (DS31783 pp. 1–2). In the T1 one part gates the privacy LED from the microphone rail MIC_1V8, so the LED cannot light without mic power (BRD-027), and a second one sits in the MaskROM combo circuit (BOM v1 lines 35 and 97). The `-7` suffix is 3000 pcs tape and reel (DS31783 p. 1).

## Power by mode

Off: IDSS ≤ 100 nA at 20 V, IGSS ≤ 1 µA at ±4.5 V (DS31783 p. 2). On: conduction loss ID² × RDS(on); for an LED current of a few mA the dissipation is negligible.

## Pinout and package

SOT523 (DS31783 p. 1, p. 6). Top view (p. 1): drain on the single lead on one side; gate and source on the two leads on the other side. Pin numbers are not printed in the datasheet; the common SOT523 convention is 1 = G, 2 = S, 3 = D (**UNVERIFIED** against this datasheet; confirm with the Diodes package-outline page before drawing the symbol).

Package dimensions (DS31783 p. 6, mm): D 1.50–1.70, E 1.45–1.75, E1 0.75–0.85, A2 0.60–0.80, b 0.15–0.30, e 0.50 BSC, e1 0.90–1.10, L 0.20–0.40. Marking code NA1 plus date code (p. 1). MSL 1 (p. 1).

## Recommended footprint

DS31783 p. 6, "Suggested Pad Layout" (mm): C 1.29, X 0.40, X1 0.70, Y 0.51, Y1 1.80. The datasheet refers to <https://www.diodes.com/package-outlines.html> for the latest version.

## Application circuit and required passives

Privacy-LED gate (BOM v1 line 35): gate on MIC_1V8, source to GND, drain to the LED cathode through its resistor, LED anode from FP_3V3 (a red LED needs about 2 V, so it cannot run from 1.8 V). With VGS = 1.8 V the RDS(on) is specified ≤ 0.7 Ω at 350 mA (DS31783 p. 2), so the switch is fully on at the mic-rail voltage. A gate pull-down keeps it off while MIC_1V8 is floating (value **UNVERIFIED**, not set in the BOM).

## Design notes for the T1

- VGS(th) max 1.0 V leaves 0.8 V of overdrive at 1.8 V gate drive at 25 °C (DS31783 p. 2). Threshold variation over temperature is plotted on p. 4 (not transcribed); check it at −20 °C before relying on the 1.8 V gate drive.
- VGSS is ±6 V; any gate tied to a rail above 5 V needs a divider or clamp.
