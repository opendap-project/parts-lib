## Summary

The IM73D122V01 is a bottom-port digital PDM MEMS microphone with 73 dB(A) SNR at 3.072 MHz clock, −26 dBFS sensitivity, 122 dBSPL acoustic overload point, IP57 at component level, and power modes selected by the PDM clock frequency (datasheet v1.00, pp. 1, 6). In the T1 two are fitted: the main microphone on the faceplate and an ambient-noise microphone on the body, both powered from the gated MIC_1V8 rail. BOM line 34.

## Recommended footprint

Infineon recommendation, solder-mask-defined pads (datasheet v1.00, §5 and Figure 13, p. 12):

- Four signal pads 0.75 mm × 0.45 mm (copper), 1.7 mm pitch across, 0.7 mm pitch along; stencil apertures 0.65 mm × 0.4 mm.
- Ground ring around the port: outer radius 1.04 mm, inner radius 0.58 mm; stencil as six segmented apertures (R1/R0.6, 0.12 mm gaps).
- PCB acoustic port: radius 0.4 mm (diameter 0.8 mm), larger than the microphone port.
- Dimensions 0.68 mm and 0.8 mm locate the port centre relative to the pad rows (Figure 13).

Reflow (Table 10, p. 14): Pb-free peak 260 °C +0/−5 °C, 20–40 s within 5 °C of peak, 60–150 s above 217 °C. Do not apply vacuum or air to the port, no ultrasonic or air-blow cleaning, use no-clean paste (p. 15).

## Application circuit and required passives

- VDD bypass: 1 µF close to the VDD pad for best SNR (Table 5 note 4, p. 7); the stereo configuration note recommends 100 nF close to VDD (§3.5, p. 10). Use the 1 µF value the table note calls for, or both: the datasheet gives both values (**UNVERIFIED** which one applies to the single-mic case).
- Optional series termination of about 100 Ω on DATA to reduce ringing (§3.5, p. 10).
- LR strap to GND or VDD (§3.5, Table 8, p. 10).

## Interfaces and timing

- **PDM:** with LR = GND the microphone drives DATA on the falling clock edge and releases it on the rising edge (channel DATA1); with LR = VDD it drives on the rising edge (DATA2) (Table 8, p. 10). Two microphones can share one DATA line and one clock; both then run in the same power mode (§3.5, p. 10).
- **Timing** (Table 6, pp. 8–9): DATA driven 40–80 ns after the clock edge (tDD); high-Z 5–30 ns after the edge (tHZ); DATA valid ≤ 100 ns (tDV, Cload 100 pF, Rload 100 kΩ). Output levels VOL ≤ 0.3·VDD, VOH ≥ 0.7·VDD.
- **Start-up:** 20 ms to ±0.5 dB, 50 ms to ±0.2 dB sensitivity after VDD and clock; same for mode switches (Table 6, p. 8). An idle tone (alternating ones and zeros) is output within 3 ms of VDD and clock (p. 9).
- **DC offset in the bit stream:** −80 dBFS with LR = GND, −40 dBFS with LR = VDD (Table 7, p. 9).
- In standby the DATA pad is high-impedance (Table 5 note 5, p. 7).

## Design notes for the T1

- Supply: MIC_1V8 (1.8 V) gated by the DMG1012T switch (BOM line 35); both microphones lose power together with the privacy LED gate (BRD-027 per the BOM note).
- 1.8 V logic on CLOCK and DATA matches the 1.8 V I/O domain of the PDM pins on the SoC side; pin-plan assignment: **UNVERIFIED** in this sheet.
- For two microphones on one PDM data line, strap one LR to GND and the other to VDD; the faceplate microphone sits behind a connector, so DATA routing length and the optional 100 Ω termination need checking against the 200 pF load limit.
- Choose the clock for SNR versus current: 3.072 MHz gives 73 dB(A) at 0.98 mA; 768 kHz gives 69 dB(A) (8 kHz band) at 0.28 mA (Tables 3 and 6).
- Port: 0.8 mm PCB hole; the case acoustic path and mesh are outside this sheet.
