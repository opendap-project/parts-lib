# 19-337/R6GHBHC-A01/2T

- **Manufacturer:** Everlight Electronics
- **Function:** Full-colour SMD LED: red (AlGaInP), green and blue (InGaN) dies, each on its own anode and cathode, water-clear resin
- **BOM v1 lines:** 79 (touch-faceplate RGB status LED, qty 1; BOM v1.2 change 7, replaces the 19-217/G7C-AN1P2/6T)
- **Package:** 6-pad SMD, 1.6 × 1.6 mm, 0.35 mm overall height
- **Datasheet:** 19-337/R6GHBHC-A01/2T, issue DSE-0002657 Rev.4 (release date 21 May 2013). Cited below as "DSE-0002657". File sha256 `20990b41beab81cbc0da25070fe15e00f4e491f6b905b12110f1e118559cee8e` (matches the BOM v1 Datasheets table).
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The 19-337/R6GHBHC-A01/2T is a full-colour SMD LED with three chips: R6 (AlGaInP, brilliant red), GH (InGaN, brilliant green) and BH (InGaN, blue), in water-clear resin, on 8 mm tape (DSE-0002657, p. 1–2). At 20 mA the dominant wavelengths are 624, 525 and 470 nm typ and VF is 1.7–2.4 V (red) and 2.7–3.8 V (green, blue) (p. 4). Each die has its own pair of pads (p. 9). In the T1 it is the developer-controllable status LED on the touch faceplate, driven from the main board's [LP5521](../Texas-Instruments/LP5521YQX-NOPB.md) current sources over LED_R, LED_G and LED_B on the faceplate connector, with the cathodes to faceplate GND (BOM v1 line 79; requirements FPT-003, FPT-016, FPT-025).

## Power by mode

Not applicable (passive emitter). Dissipation per die is VF × IF; at the T1 cap of 20 mA: red ≤ 2.5 V × 20 mA = 50 mW, green and blue ≤ 3.9 V × 20 mA = 78 mW, within the 60 / 95 mW ratings at 25 °C (p. 3, p. 4). ## Recommended footprint

"Recommend soldering pad" (DSE-0002657, p. 9): six pads in two columns of three, with a central keep-out bar 0.8 mm wide; the drawing gives an overall 2.2 ± 0.2 mm width, 1.9 ± 0.3 mm and 1.35 ± 0.2 mm across the pad columns, pad widths 0.55 ± 0.15 mm, pad heights 0.4 ± 0.2 mm (outer rows) and 0.5 ± 0.1 mm (middle row), and 0.725 ± 0.1 mm between outer and middle pad references. Which pad edges several of these dimensions reference is not clear from the drawing (**UNVERIFIED**: confirm against the PDF when drawing the KiCad footprint). The datasheet says the pads are for reference only and may be modified.

## Application circuit and required passives

- Current limiting is mandatory: "Customer must apply resistors for protection" (p. 12, §1). In the T1 the LP5521 constant current sources limit the current, so no series resistor is fitted (BOM v1 line 79). Whether a constant-current drive satisfies the datasheet's resistor instruction is a design judgement, not a datasheet statement.
- Reflow at most twice, Pb-free profile with 260 °C max for 10 s (p. 12, §3). Hand soldering below 350 °C for 3 s per terminal; repair after soldering is not recommended (p. 12, §4–5).

## Design notes for the T1

- **VF and the driver supply (BOM v1.2 change 7):** the BOM uses VF ≤ 3.8 V for green and blue and adds the ±0.1 V tolerance (3.9 V worst case); both figures match p. 4. The headroom check against the LP5521 charge pump is in the [LP5521 sheet](../Texas-Instruments/LP5521YQX-NOPB.md#design-notes-for-the-t1).
- **Current cap:** the BOM caps each channel at 20 mA, 80 % of IF 25 mA (p. 3). The 25 mA rating is at Ta = 25 °C; the derating curves (pp. 6–8) apply at higher ambient inside the case, so the 20 mA cap must be checked against them at the case's worst internal temperature (**UNVERIFIED**). The expected indicator level of 2–5 mA (BOM line 79) is far below.
- **Brightness at the drive point:** Iv is specified only at 20 mA (p. 4); the relative-intensity curves (pp. 6–8, typical) must be read or the LED measured on a sample through the case window (**UNVERIFIED**). Red, green and blue intensities differ by bin (p. 5), so colour mixing needs per-channel calibration.
- **ESD:** 150 V HBM for green and blue (p. 3). The faceplate low-speed ESD array (BOM v1 line 75) on LED_R, LED_G and LED_B is required, as the BOM states.
- **Reverse voltage** 5 V max (p. 3): the LP5521 sources only, so no reverse bias arises in normal operation.
- The -M01 variant named in the BOM as second source has not been checked against this drawing (**UNVERIFIED**).
- Topr −40 to +85 °C (p. 3).
