## Summary

Red 0603 SMD LED from Everlight's 19-217 family, used as the microphone privacy LED on the faceplate (BOM v1 line 80, requirement BRD-027). It is hardware-tied to the microphone power: the same switch that powers the microphones (BOM v1 line 35, DMG1012T) switches it, so software cannot turn the microphones on without lighting it.

## Design notes for the T1

- The privacy function depends on the LED and the microphones sharing one switched rail. Keep the LED on the load side of the switch, so any path that powers the microphones also lights it.
- Which rail feeds the LED is not stated in the BOM. If it hangs on the 1.8 V microphone supply (MIC_1V8, BOM line 71), the LED's VF may leave too little headroom to light it. Check this first when the datasheet is obtained (**UNVERIFIED**).
