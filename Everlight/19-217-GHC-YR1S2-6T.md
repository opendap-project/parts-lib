## Summary

The 19-217/GHC-YR1S2/6T is a 0603 green InGaN SMD LED (DSE-0025583 p. 1–2). In the T1 it is the developer-controllable status LED on the touch faceplate, driven from PWM9 M0 (GPIO 3_B2, 3.3 V bank) through the faceplate connector (BOM v1 line 79; requirements FPT-003, FPT-016).

## Power by mode

Set by the drive current chosen in the schematic: on-state dissipation is IF × VF, for example 20 mA × 3.3 V typ = 66 mW against the 95 mW Pd limit (p. 2–3). Off: reverse leakage only.

## Design notes for the T1

- **Headroom:** VF is 2.7–3.7 V at 20 mA (p. 3), so a 3.3 V rail or GPIO cannot drive 20 mA with a series resistor across the VF spread. The T1 needs a much lower current (the curve on p. 5 gives VF at lower IF, typical only) or a drive from a higher rail through a transistor or sink. Pick the operating current and verify VF at that current on samples (**UNVERIFIED** until measured).
- The ESD rating is only 150 V HBM (p. 2). The LED sits on the faceplate near a user-touchable surface, so keep it behind the faceplate low-speed ESD protection (line 75) and handle it as ESD-sensitive at assembly.
- Topr is −40 to +85 °C (p. 2).
