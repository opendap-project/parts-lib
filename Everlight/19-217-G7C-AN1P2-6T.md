# 19-217/G7C-AN1P2/6T

- **Manufacturer:** Everlight Electronics
- **Function:** SMD LED, brilliant yellow green (AlGaInP), water-clear resin
- **BOM v1 lines:** 79 (touch-faceplate status LED, qty 1; BOM v1.1, replaces the 19-217/GHC-YR1S2/6T)
- **Package:** 0603 (1.6 × 0.8 × 0.4 mm)
- **Datasheet:** 19-217/G7C-AN1P2/6T, issue DSE-0014851 Rev.4 (release date 16 December 2021). Cited below as "DSE-0014851". File sha256 `9329ea0e5aeb9d40f738f4bc8824cc6f3a6b63ab2bc7fe237d06fa69fac017d5` (matches the BOM v1.1 Datasheets table).
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The 19-217/G7C-AN1P2/6T is a 0603 mono-colour SMD LED with an AlGaInP chip, brilliant yellow-green emission and water-clear resin (DSE-0014851 p. 1–2). Peak wavelength 575 nm typ, dominant wavelength 569.5–577.5 nm, VF 1.7–2.4 V at 20 mA (p. 3). In the T1 it is the developer-controllable status LED on the touch faceplate, driven from PWM9 M0 (GPIO 3_B2, 3.3 V bank) through the faceplate connector and a 270 Ω series resistor (BOM v1 line 79; requirements FPT-003, FPT-016). It replaces the InGaN green part, whose VF up to 3.7 V could not be driven from 3.3 V (BOM v1 §9 D16).

## Power by mode

Set by the drive current: on-state dissipation is IF × VF. At the T1 drive (≤ 5.9 mA, see Design notes) and VF ≤ 2.4 V the dissipation is below 15 mW, against the 60 mW Pd limit (p. 2). Off: reverse leakage only (≤ 10 µA at 5 V, p. 3); in the T1 the LED is never reverse-biased.

## Application circuit and required passives

- A series current-limiting resistor is mandatory (p. 9, Precautions §1). In the T1: 270 Ω from the 3.3 V PWM pin (BOM v1 line 79).
- Reflow at most twice, 260 °C maximum for 10 s, with the Pb-free profile on p. 9. Repair after soldering is not recommended (p. 9).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Drive current check (BOM v1 line 79):** I = (3.3 V − VF) / 270 Ω with the 20 mA VF figures (p. 3): 4.8 mA at VF typ 2.0 V and 3.3 mA at VF max 2.4 V, as the BOM states; 5.9 mA at VF min 1.7 V. These use VF at 20 mA; at 3–6 mA the VF is lower (p. 5, IF–VF curve, typical: conduction starts near 1.8 V), so the real current is somewhat higher. The GPIO output high level under load is taken as 3.3 V; the RK3566 VOH at this current is **UNVERIFIED**. All cases stay far below the 25 mA rating.
- **Brightness at the drive point:** Iv is specified only at 20 mA (28.5–72 mcd, p. 3); at about 5 mA the relative-intensity curve (p. 5, typical) must be read or the LED measured on a sample (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Colour:** yellow-green (575 nm typ), not green. The colour change is for the maintainers to accept (BOM v1 §7 item 11).
- ESD rating 2000 V HBM (p. 2). The LED still sits behind the faceplate low-speed ESD protection (BOM v1 line 75).
- Topr −40 to +85 °C (p. 2).
