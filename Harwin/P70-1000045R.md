# P70-1000045R

- **Manufacturer:** Harwin
- **Function:** SMT spring-loaded pogo pin, gold-plated, tape and reel
- **Package:** Cylindrical SMT pin, Ø 2.00 mm flange, 3.50 mm free height
- **Datasheet:** Customer Information Sheet, drawing P70-10X0045R, sheet 2 of 2, issue 4, 02.07.21 (copy obtained from the LCSC datasheet mirror, part C17702549)
- **BOM v1 line:** 67 (about 128 pieces, on the HAT device-side piece)

## Summary

The P70-1000045R is the shortest member of Harwin's P70-10X0045R family of SMT spring-loaded pins, rated 2.0 A with 30 mΩ maximum contact resistance (drawing P70-10X0045R, Specification). In the T1 project the pins form the HAT contact field on the device-side piece (board ADR 0002, hat-standard scope); the board BOM keeps the line for traceability only (BOM v1 line 67).

## Design notes for the T1

- Working stroke from maximum to minimum working height is 0.30 mm (3.00 → 2.70 mm, size table); the design target is the normal working height, 2.90 mm, where the 0.88 N force is specified.
- At about 128 pins and 0.88 N ± 0.29 N each, the total spring force at normal height is about 113 N (range about 76–150 N), computed from the drawing's figures; the HAT retention and the case must carry it.
- 2.0 A per pin (drawing): VHAT and ground need several pins each, sized against the HAT power budget.
- 10,000 cycles (drawing) bounds HAT swap life.
- High-speed behaviour of the pin is unpublished; a test coupon is needed with the DF40 flat cable (BOM line 66) before freeze.
