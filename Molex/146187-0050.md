- **Manufacturer:** Molex
- **Function:** 2.4/5 GHz balanced dual-band PCB antenna with 1.13 mm coaxial cable and U.FL-compatible plug
- **BOM v1 lines:** 57 (Wi-Fi/Bluetooth antennas), quantity 2
- **Package:** Rigid PCB 40.95 × 9.00 × 0.70 mm, cable length 43.3 mm
- **Datasheet:** Product design drawing (sales drawing) 1461870050, series 146187, sheet 1 of 1, drawn 2017/09/27, EC no. 122478, revision D (copy obtained from the LCSC datasheet mirror, part C6390118)
- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The 146187 is a balanced dual-band 2.4/5 GHz antenna on a 0.7 mm rigid PCB, delivered with a soldered Ø 1.13 mm coaxial cable ending in a 2.5 mm-high U.FL connector (drawing 1461870050, title and note 1). The -0050 variant has a 43.3 mm cable (drawing, item table). In the T1 two of them serve the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth module (BOM v1 line 57), connected through U.FL receptacles (line 56).

The BOM calls this part a "2.4/5 GHz FPC" antenna; the drawing describes a rigid PCB antenna, 0.70 mm thick (note 1).

## Design notes for the T1

- The cable must not be lifted in the vertical direction at the antenna end (note 2): route it flat from the antenna PCB.
- Placement depends on the case material (BOM line 57); a 40.95 × 9.00 mm keep-out per antenna away from metal is needed in the case design (size from the drawing; clearance distance **UNVERIFIED** until the application specification is read).
- The U.FL plug is 2.5 mm high (note 1) and mates the U.FL-R-SMT-1 receptacle (line 56).
- Choose the cable-length variant (item table) once the module and antenna positions are fixed; -0050 is the shortest.
- Note (2026-10-24): Molex names its variants by a nominal length class (-0050 = 50 mm) and draws the bare cable about 6.7 mm shorter (43.3 mm here; the same in drawing 1461531050 for the 146153, and L1 = 43 / L2 = 50 mm in drawing 2196110100 for the 219611). Murata's "50 mm or more" for Japan therefore most likely means this length class, not the bare cable (**UNVERIFIED** until Murata confirms). Board ADR 0018 replaces this antenna in BOM line 57 with the Molex [146153-0050](146153-0050.md), which is on the FCC, ISED and Japan lists; the 146187 stays a Japan-only alternative.
