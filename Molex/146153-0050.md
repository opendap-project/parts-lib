## Summary

The 146153 is a balanced, dual-band dipole on a 0.1 mm polyimide flex, 34.9 × 9.0 mm, with a soldered Ø 1.13 mm cable ending in a plug (SD 1461531050, note 1). The -00xx variants carry a U.FL / I-PEX MHF1 plug (Digi-Key description "U.FL (UMCC), I-PEX MHF1", vendor claim); the -1xxx variants carry an I-PEX MHF4L plug (SD 1461531050, note 1). The -0050 is the 50 mm length class.

It is on the antenna lists of the Murata Type 2EL certification for the USA (FCC Table 20), Canada (ISED Table 36, "146153 (50 mm cable)") and Japan (Table 56), at 3.2 dBi (2.4 GHz) and 4.25 dBi (5 GHz) peak, dipole, u.FL (CER-0248 pp. 26, 53, 68). Murata requires a cable of "50 mm or more" (E2B-09-1413 Table 3, p. 14). In the T1 it is the single antenna on the module's ANT0, shared by Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (board ADR 0018).

## Recommended footprint

Not applicable: the antenna is not soldered to the board. It sticks to a non-metallic surface of the case and plugs into a U.FL receptacle (Hirose U.FL-R-SMT-1, BOM line 56) at the end of Murata's copied RF trace.

## Application circuit and required passives

A π matching position between the module RF pin and the U.FL, in 0603 as in Murata's reference (E2B-09-1413 Figures 9–10), tuned on the final enclosure. Tuning must keep the gain at or below the registered 3.2 / 4.25 dBi and spurious emissions within 3 dB of Murata's report (CER-0248 §2.1.5.1, §2.1.3.12).

## Design notes for the T1

- Position: inside of the top wall, long axis along the width, away from the microSD corner; the 9.0 mm width does not fit flat in the 7.0 mm top band (board research *Antennas and RF exposure* §3).
- Keep-out: provisional ≥ 5 mm from the radiating element to any metal (battery, pocket floor, microSD shell, inserts, HAT metal), **UNVERIFIED** until the application specification is read and the prototype is measured.
- The U.FL sits in the top band near the top wall so the 50 mm-class cable reaches it.
- SAR: the T1's separations are below 5 mm; SAR testing is needed in the USA, Canada and the EU whatever the placement (board ADR 0018).
