## Summary

Micro-Fit 3.0 is Molex's 3.00 mm pitch wire-to-board power connector family with a positive latch. The 43650-0400 is the 4-circuit, single-row, right-angle through-hole header (Digi-Key listing: "Connector Header Through Hole, Right Angle 4 position 0.118" (3.00 mm)"). In the T1 it is the battery connector of the two-cell pack (board ADR 0014 item 1): P+, P−, the mid-cell tap for the gauge, and the pack NTC. Its keyed housing cannot take a 1S JST-PH lead, so a 1S pack cannot be fitted by mistake.

## Application circuit and required passives

None. In the T1 the P+ contact feeds the 5 A fuse (board BOM line 104) and the BQ25798 BAT pins; P− returns to ground through the 5 mΩ low-side gauge sense resistor (line 43); the tap goes to the BQ28Z610-R1 VC1; the NTC to the BQ25798 TS.

## Design notes for the T1

- **Margin (BRD-049):** the worst continuous case, ≈ 3.2 A (full HAT/USB-C budget with full-scale headphones, board ADR 0014), is 64 % of 5 A per contact with the 43030-0001 crimp (2.4 A at the full budget = 48 %; ≤ 70 % connector rule). The pack lead and plug must be rated ≥ 4.6 A per pole (board ADR 0014 item 2).
- **Orientation:** right angle, as in the high-current study, so the connector band sits outside the battery pocket; the space it needs at the pocket edge is **UNVERIFIED** (board 2S study §6).
- **Variants:** 43650-0403 (Mouser 6,411) and 43650-0404 (Mouser 101) are other members of the same series; how they differ from -0400 (finish, pegs) is **UNVERIFIED**.
