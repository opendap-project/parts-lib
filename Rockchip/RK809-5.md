# RK809-5

- **Manufacturer:** Rockchip
- **Function:** Power-management IC: five synchronous bucks, nine LDOs, two power switches, RTC with 32.768 kHz output, battery fuel gauge, audio codec with headphone and class-D drivers
- **Package:** QFN68, 7 × 7 mm, 0.35 mm pitch, exposed ground pad
- **Datasheet:** RK809 Datasheet, Revision 1.01 (2018-08-28), <https://rockchip.fr/RK809%20datasheet%20V1.01.pdf>, sha256 `3f9b42ed70ec…` in `manifest.toml`
- **BOM v1 lines:** 2 (PMIC), 5 (BUCK1–5 inductors), 49 (rails inside the PMIC), 99 (faceplate VCI on LDO4)

## Summary

The RK809 is a PMIC with five configurable synchronous step-down converters, nine LDOs, two switches, a battery fuel gauge, an RTC with a 32.768 kHz output buffer, and an audio codec with a headphone driver and a class-D driver; its power-up and power-down sequence and default voltages are OTP-programmed (Rev 1.01 p. 8, §1.1). In the T1 it is the main PMIC next to the RK3566 (BOM v1 line 2, *Proposed*): BUCK1 vdd_logic, BUCK2 vdd_gpu, BUCK3 vcc_ddr, BUCK4 vdd_npu, BUCK5 vcc_1v8, LDO1–9 for PHY, PMU, faceplate VCI and microSD rails, SWOUT1 for the Wi-Fi module and SWOUT2 for microSD, EXT_EN enabling the external vcc3v3_sys buck (power tree v0 §2, §3). The codec, class-D driver and fuel gauge are not used (BOM v1 line 2; the gauge is the MAX17055, line 42).

**Variant.** The datasheet covers the RK809 family and lists only **RK809-1** in its ordering table and marking (p. 12, §2.1, §2.2). Its default-voltage and sequence table is for RK809-1 (pp. 22–23, Table 4-1). **The RK809-5 OTP defaults and sequence are not in this document: UNVERIFIED.** Everything below that is not OTP-dependent (ratings, pinout, package, electrical characteristics) is taken as common to the family, which is itself an assumption the document does not state explicitly (**UNVERIFIED**).

## Design notes for the T1

- **OTP variant.** All defaults in the datasheet are RK809-1. RK809-1 starts BUCK4 at 3.0 V and BUCK5 at 3.3 V (Table 4-1); the T1 uses BUCK4 for vdd_npu (0.9 V) and BUCK5 for vcc_1v8 (1.8 V) (power tree v0 §3). Those RK809-1 defaults would be destructive on those rails, so the RK809-5 OTP table must be confirmed before the rail mapping is frozen (power tree v0 §6.1, open question 4, risk 2).
- **SWOUT ratings.** The DC table gives SWOUT1 1.5 A and SWOUT2 3 A (p. 19). Power tree v0 §3 lists vcc3v3_wl (SWOUT1) as a 3 A switch and vcc3v3_sd (SWOUT2) as 1.5 A: the two values are swapped against the datasheet. The Wi-Fi rail (0.6 A est.) still fits within 1.5 A.
- **Switch supplies.** VCC9 supplies BUCK5 **and** SWOUT1; VCC8 supplies SWOUT2 (p. 15). In Figs. 1-1/1-2 VCC8 comes from BUCK5. In the T1, BUCK5 is 1.8 V, so VCC8 must be tied to vcc3v3_sys (not BUCK5) for SWOUT2 to give 3.3 V to the microSD. The power tree names only VCC9 ← vcc3v3_sys.
- **VCC9 on 3.3 V.** VCC9 recommended range is 3–5.5 V, typ 5 V (p. 16). Feeding it from vcc3v3_sys (power tree v0 §2) leaves 0.3 V of margin to the minimum, and makes BUCK5 (sequence slot 1 on RK809-1) depend on EXT_EN and the TLV62569 coming up first. Fig. 1-2 shows the intended pattern: EXT_EN enables the external buck that makes VCC_SYS. The order of EXT_EN against slot 1 on the RK809-5 is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **LDO4–6 supply (VCC6).** In Figs. 1-1/1-2 VCC6 and VCC7 come from BUCK5. With BUCK5 = 1.8 V in the T1, LDO4 (FP_VCI 2.8 V), LDO5 (vccio_sd up to 3.3 V) and LDO6 (vcc3v3_pmu 3.3 V) need VCC6 above 3.3 V plus dropout; no dropout is specified. VCC6's source must come from the reference board (Orange Pi 3B) schematic (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **LDO4 for FP_VCI (line 99).** 400 mA rated at VIN 3.6 V / VOUT 3.3 V, 300 mA at VIN 2 V / VOUT 1.8 V (p. 18); Table 4-1 says 100 mA. The panel VCI load is small either way, but the 100 mA figure is the safe design limit until resolved.
- **I2C.** Address 0x20, 1 MHz max, VIH 0.7 × VCC_1P8D: compatible with I2C0 at 1.8 V (power tree v0 finding 11).
- **EXT_EN** is a push-pull output at VCC_RTC level (VOH2, p. 20), not open drain. Check it against the TLV62569 EN absolute maximum (VIN level) when VCC_RTC sits on VSYS.
- **VCC_RTC must be the highest voltage on the RK809** (Fig. 1-1 note, p. 10).
- **Gauge unused.** The RK809 gauge pins (SNSP, SNSN, BATDIV) are not used in the T1 (MAX17055 instead). Their termination when unused is not stated (**UNVERIFIED**; follow the reference board).
- **Codec and class-D unused** (BOM v1 line 2). Termination of VCC_SPK_HP, the charge-pump pins and audio pins when unused is not stated (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **VDC** starts the PMIC on its first rise above 0.55 V (p. 15). Whether the T1 ties it to a VBUS divider (auto power-on on charger insertion) is a design choice not yet made.
- **Thermal.** 2 W maximum dissipation and θJA 21.99 °C/W on a 4-layer test board (p. 101); the T1's sealed body is not modelled (power tree v0 risk 10).
- **Inductors (line 5).** The Murata DFE252012F-R47M saturation rating against 4 A remains **UNVERIFIED** (BOM v1 line 5).
