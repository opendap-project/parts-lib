## Summary

The RK3566 is a quad-core Arm Cortex-A55 application processor with a Mali-G52-2EE GPU, a 1 TOPS NPU, a 32-bit DDR4/DDR3/DDR3L/LPDDR4/LPDDR4X/LPDDR3 controller, eMMC 5.1 and SFC, 4K decode / 1080p encode, HDMI 2.0, eDP 1.3, dual MIPI-DSI, MIPI CSI-2, and the audio interfaces I2S0/I2S1 (8 channels), I2S2/I2S3 (2 channels), SPDIF, PDM (8 channels), TDM (8 channels) and VAD, in a 15.5 × 14.4 mm FCCSP565L package (RK3566 Brief, p. 1). In the T1 it is the main SoC (BOM v1 line 1): it runs OpenDAP OS, drives the two CS43198 DACs over I2S, reads the PDM microphones, and reaches every chip directly (BRD-004).

## Interfaces and timing

From the RK3566 Brief p. 1 unless noted; details from RK3568 DS Rev 1.0 where the RK3566 has the same block (**UNVERIFIED** for exact equivalence):

- **Memory:** 32-bit DDR4/DDR3/DDR3L/LPDDR4/LPDDR4X/LPDDR3; LPDDR4X up to 3200, up to 8 GB for LPDDR4/4X (RK3568 DS Rev 1.0, p. 8, §1.2.5). eMMC 4.41–5.1, 1/4/8-bit, HS200, command queue; SD 3.0 / MMC 4.51, 4-bit (p. 8).
- **Boot:** BootROM boots from SPI flash, NAND, eMMC or SDMMC; download over USB OTG (device) (RK3568 DS Rev 1.0, p. 8, §1.2.4).
- **Audio:** I2S0 8 channels (HDMI only on RK3568), I2S1 8 channels TX/RX with I2S and PCM formats, I2S2/I2S3 2 channels; 16–32-bit; up to 192 kHz; master or slave; PDM up to 8 channels, 16–24-bit, up to 192 kHz; TDM 8 channels (RK3568 DS Rev 1.0, p. 15, §1.2.14). The DSD capability of the I2S/TDM blocks is not stated (**UNVERIFIED**; the DSD128 limit is in opendap-os ADR 0006 per BOM v1 line 18).
- **Display:** single display engine; HDMI 2.0, eDP 1.3, dual MIPI-DSI combined with single LVDS, 24-bit RGB/BT1120, EBC. Maximum MIPI DSI lane rate on RK3566 **UNVERIFIED** (BOM v1 §4).
- **Camera:** 8 MP ISP, MIPI CSI-2 1 × 4 lanes or 2 × 2 lanes, DVP.
- **Connectivity:** one USB 3.0 host, two SATA 3.0, one PCIe 2.1 on two shared SerDes lanes; two USB 2.0 hosts and one USB 2.0 OTG; one RGMII.
- **Low-speed:** 6 × I²C, 10 × UART, 4 × SPI, 16 × PWM, 4 × SARADC, up to 142 GPIO (RK3566 Brief, p. 2, block diagram).
- **PLLs:** Frac PLL lock time 250 typ / 500 max input-clock cycles, Int PLL 1000 / 1500 cycles, both at FREF = 24 MHz (RK3568 DS Rev 1.0, p. 58, Tables 3-5, 3-6).

## Design notes for the T1

- **PMUIO1 is 3.3 V only** (2.97–3.63 V; RK3568 DS Rev 1.0, p. 55). PMUIO2 and VCCIO1–7 accept 1.8 or 3.3 V. The T1 sets PMUIO2 (I2C0/1/2) to 1.8 V and VCCIO5 (faceplate) to 3.3 V (BOM v1 §0 item 7).
- **LPDDR4X VDDQ window** 0.54–0.66 V (p. 55) drives the TPS7A1006 choice (line 9).
- **Ambient range 0–80 °C** (p. 56) is narrower than the industrial ranges of the support parts; it bounds the T1 thermal design.
- **Audio clocks:** the CS43198 DACs take their MCLK from the dedicated oscillators (lines 20, 21, ADR 0010), not from an RK3566 PLL. Whether the RK3566 I2S runs as slave to the DAC-side clock or as master from an external MCLK input is a pin-plan decision; the I2S blocks support both modes (p. 15).
- Anything about the RK3566 package, ball map, IO-domain-per-ball or power-up sequence must come from the RK3566 hardware design guide or the Radxa ZERO 3W schematic, not from the RK3568 datasheet.
