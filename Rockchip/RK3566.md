## Summary

The RK3566 is a quad-core Arm Cortex-A55 application processor with a Mali-G52-2EE GPU, a 1 TOPS NPU, a 32-bit DDR3/DDR3L/DDR4/LPDDR3/LPDDR4/LPDDR4X controller, eMMC 5.1 and FSPI, 4K decode / 1080p encode, HDMI 2.0, eDP 1.3, dual MIPI-DSI, MIPI CSI-2, and the audio interfaces I2S1 (8 channels), I2S2/I2S3 (2 channels), PDM (8 channels), SPDIF and VAD, in a 15.5 × 14.4 mm FCCSP565L package (RK3566 Brief, p. 1; DS 1.1, pp. 18–19). In the T1 it is the main SoC (BOM v1 line 1): it runs Blackbird OS, drives the two CS43198 DACs over I2S, reads the PDM microphones, and reaches every chip directly (BRD-004).

## Pinout and package

- **Package** FCCSP565L, body 15.5 × 14.4 mm (DS 1.1, p. 21, Fig. 2-2). Height A 0.762 / 0.833 / 0.904 mm, stand-off A1 0.120 / 0.160 / 0.200 mm, A2 0.593 / 0.673 / 0.731 mm, ball diameter b 0.200 / 0.250 / 0.300 mm (min / nom / max; p. 22, Fig. 2-5). Coplanarity 0.08 mm; ball position tolerance Ø0.15 mm to C-A-B and Ø0.05 mm to C (p. 21, Fig. 2-3; p. 22, Fig. 2-4). The feature list gives "ball size 0.3 mm; ball pitch 0.65 & 0.4 mm" (p. 18); the brief's "0.4 mm + 0.6 mm" (RK3566 Brief, p. 1) is superseded by the drawing's 0.650.
- **Ball field** (DS 1.1, p. 21, Fig. 2-3, and the pin list pp. 23–30, counted in this session):
  - Outermost ball centres span 14.800 × 13.600 mm. The edge-to-first-ball distance is 0.350 mm (x) and 0.400 mm (y); edge to the first core ball is 1.575 mm (x) and 1.675 mm (y).
  - **Core**: 343 balls named `1A1`…`1V20` (18 rows × 20 columns, partly depopulated) on a 0.65 mm grid.
  - **Perimeter ring**: 222 balls named `A1`…`AR38` on a 0.4 mm grid in two rows per side (38 columns × 35 rows). The inner row of the ring (row B, row AP, columns 2 and 37) is fully populated; the outer row (row A 24/38, row AR 25/38, column 1 20/35, column 38 23/35) has about one ball in three missing.
  - The ring holds most of the DDR byte lanes, eMMC data, USB, PCIe, the MIPI DSI/CSI and HDMI pairs, eDP, the 24 MHz crystal, nPOR and part of the audio buses (for example I2S1_M1 SDO0 on AN2 and SCLK_TX on core ball 1U1; I2S3_M0 SCLK/LRCK/MCLK/SDO on AP10/AR9/AR10/AP9). The full ball map is DS 1.1, p. 23, Fig. 2-6, and the pin list is on pp. 23–30.
- **Power and ground balls**: DS 1.1, pp. 30–31, Table 2-2. DDR supplies: DDR_VDDQ_1–8 on 1D5–1D9, 1E4, 1F4, 1G4; DDR_VDDQL_1–5 on 1E6, 1E7, 1E9, 1F5, 1G5; DDR_AVSS on 1D4 (HDG 1.1, p. 23, Fig. 2-8).

## Recommended footprint

Rockchip publishes **no land pattern**: DS 1.1 gives the package drawing only (pp. 21–22), and HDG 1.1 and HSPCB 1.0 give no pad or via size for the SoC. Land and via sizes are a T1 choice derived from the drawing: ball Ø 0.25 mm nominal, ring pitch 0.4 mm, core pitch 0.65 mm. The only Rockchip via figure found is for the RK3568 LPDDR4X core-board template: 0.2 mm hole, 0.4 mm pad (template design notes, item 5.2). The escape analysis is in the [board research note `2026-10-rk3566-layout-rules.md`](https://github.com/opendap-project/board/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-rk3566-layout-rules.md) §3.

The T1's land pattern is in kicad-lib, `Rockchip:Rockchip_FCCSP-565L_15.5x14.4mm_P0.4mm_P0.65mm_Ball0.25mm_Pad0.25mm_NSMD`: NSMD 0.25 mm copper on both grids, 1 mil mask per side; the reasoning is in kicad-lib [`generator/bga/README.md`](https://github.com/opendap-project/kicad-lib/blob/main/generator/bga/README.md).

Reflow (HDG 1.1, p. 99, Table 5-1, SAC305): ramp ≤ 3 °C/s; soak 150–180 °C for 60–90 s; 60–70 s above 220 °C; peak 245 ± 5 °C for 15–30 s; cooling ≤ 3 °C/s. MSL 3 (JLCPCB part page).

## Application circuit and required passives

- **Power**: RK809-5 PMIC (line 2), RK860-0 vdd_cpu buck with I²C DVS (line 3), TLV62569 3.3 V system buck (line 6), TPS7A1006 LPDDR4X 0.6 V LDO (line 9).
- **Power-up order** (HDG 1.1, p. 34): VDDA0V9_PMU / VDDA_0V9 / VDD_LOGIC → VCCA1V8_PMU / VCC_1V8 / VCC3V3_PMU / VDD_GPU → VDD_CPU → VCC_DDR → VCC_3V3 → RESETn. RESETn is released at least 10 ms after the last supply is stable.
- **Power-down** (HDG 1.1, p. 34): when PMUIO1 falls below 2.93 V, RESETn is pulled low first.
- **Supplies kept on in standby** (p. 35): VCC_DDR, VDDA0V9_PMU, VCCA1V8_PMU, VCC3V3_PMU.
- **Clock** (HDG 1.1, pp. 16–17, Fig. 2-1):
  - 24 MHz crystal, preferably a 4-pin SMT part with both GND pins grounded.
  - Load capacitors 2 × 18 pF C0G 0402. The value is matched to the crystal's CL; 18 pF suits the crystal Rockchip uses and is "not a general value".
  - 22 Ω series resistor in XOUT24M (must be kept) and 1 MΩ across XIN24M–XOUT24M (do not change).
  - An external oscillator instead: 1.8 V peak-to-peak into XIN24M, ±20 ppm, XOUT24M left open.
  - The T1 crystal (YXC X322524MOB4SI, line 15) needs its CL checked against these capacitors.
- **Reset**: nPOR is active low, minimum 100 cycles of 24 MHz (≥ 4 µs). It needs 100 nF near the pin and a pull-up to the PMUIO1 level (HDG 1.1, p. 17).
- **Boot straps**: FLASH_VOL_SEL is high for a 1.8 V VCCIO2 and low for 3.3 V. SDMMC0_DET low selects SDMMC, high selects JTAG/UART (HDG 1.1, pp. 19–20, Table 2-3).
- **DDR**:
  - With LPDDR4/4X, DDR_RZQ is a 120 Ω 1 % pull-up to VCC_DDR (HDG 1.1, p. 25). DDR_VREFOUT is not used with LPDDR4/4X.
  - LPDDR4/4X DQ and CA order cannot be swapped; it must follow the reference schematic (p. 22).
- **eMMC** (HDG 1.1, p. 26, Table 2-5): CLK has 22 Ω in series at the SoC; D0 and CMD have 10 kΩ pull-ups; reserve a pull-down on DATA_STROBE. VDDi takes 2.2 µF (or more if the eMMC asks).
- **microSD (SDMMC0)** (HDG 1.1, p. 52, Table 2-11): 22 Ω in series on DATA, CMD and CLK, with the CLK resistor near the SoC; 100 Ω in series on DET. ESD parts below 1 pF.
- **USB** (HDG 1.1, p. 59, Table 2-13): 2.2 Ω in series on the OTG0 and host D± lines. 100 nF in series on SSTX.
- **I2S** (HDG 1.1, pp. 70–71, Tables 2-20, 2-21): 22 Ω in series on MCLK, SCLK and LRCK at the source; SDO and SDI connected directly.
- **Remote sense**: VDD_CPU, VDD_LOGIC, VDD_GPU and VDD_NPU each have a feedback line from under the SoC, with 100 Ω and 1 µF at the DC-DC side (HDG 1.1, p. 38).
- **Decoupling per domain** (HDG 1.1, pp. 35–41, Figs. 2-17 to 2-26):

| Domain | Near the SoC | Near the source |
|---|---|---|
| VDD_CPU | 2 × 4.7 µF 0402, 1 × 22 µF 0603 | 2 × 22 µF 0603 |
| VDD_LOGIC | 1 × 100 nF 0201, 3 × 4.7 µF 0402, 1 × 22 µF 0603 | 10 µF, 22 µF 0603 |
| VDD_GPU | 100 nF 0201, 4.7 µF 0402, 22 µF 0603 | 10 µF, 22 µF 0603 |
| VDD_NPU | 100 nF 0201, 22 µF 0603 | 10 µF, 22 µF 0603 |
| DDR_VDDQ (VCC_DDR), LPDDR4X | 3 × 100 nF 0201, 4.7 µF 0402, 10 µF 0603 | — |
| DDR_VDDQL (VCC0V6_DDR), LPDDR4X | 3 × 100 nF 0201, 2 × 4.7 µF 0402 | — |
| SYSPLL 0.9 V / 1.8 V | 1 µF 0402 + 100 nF 0201 each | — |
| Each GPIO supply pin | 100 nF | — |

  The DDR capacitors "should be placed under the U1000 package" (Fig. 2-26). The large capacitors of CPU, LOGIC, GPU, NPU and DDR go on the back of the board under the SoC, to keep ripple below 60 mV (pp. 36–39). Rockchip asks that none of these capacitors be removed.

## Interfaces and timing

From DS 1.1 and HDG 1.1:

- **Memory:** 32-bit DDR3/DDR3L/DDR4/LPDDR3/LPDDR4/LPDDR4X; with LPDDR3/4/4X, 4 ranks and up to 8 GB (HDG 1.1, p. 21). Most official templates run up to 1056 MHz; the reachable rate depends on the layout (p. 26).
- **Storage:** eMMC 4.41–5.1, 1/4/8-bit, HS200, command queue (HDG 1.1, p. 26). Three SDMMC controllers, SD 3.01 / MMC 4.51 (p. 52).
- **Boot order:** FSPI NOR, FSPI NAND, NAND, eMMC, SDMMC; MaskROM download over USB OTG0 (HDG 1.1, p. 18).
- **Audio:**
  - I2S1 8 channels, with separate TX/RX clocks, I2S and PCM formats, master or slave. I2S1_M0 is on VCCIO1 (shared SDO/SDI balls); I2S1_M1 is on VCCIO6 (all signals available).
  - I2S2 and I2S3 have 2 channels each.
  - Up to 192 kHz, 16–32 bit (HDG 1.1, pp. 69–71).
  - PDM 8 channels, SPDIF TX.
  - DSD capability is not stated (**UNVERIFIED**; see blackbird-os ADR 0006 per BOM v1 line 18).
- **Display:**
  - Two MIPI-DSI controllers, 4 lanes each, up to 2.5 Gb/s per lane, up to 1920 × 1080 at 60 Hz (HDG 1.1, p. 77). LVDS shares DSI0, up to 1280 × 800 at 60 Hz (p. 78).
  - HDMI 2.0 and eDP 1.3.
- **Camera:** MIPI CSI-2, 4 lanes or 2 + 2 lanes (HDG 1.1, pp. 83–85).
- **Multi-PHY** (HDG 1.1, pp. 86–89):
  - Two multi-PHYs. The allowed pairs are USB 3.0 + PCIe 2.0, USB 3.0 + SATA, SATA + PCIe and SATA + SATA (Table 2-35).
  - PCIe 2.1 x1, Root Complex only, 5 GT/s.
  - CLKREQn and WAKEn must use the dedicated pins of one mux group.
- **USB:** one USB 2.0 OTG, three USB 2.0 hosts and one USB 3.0 host; full use of USB 3.0 occupies one USB 2.0 host (HDG 1.1, p. 54).
- **Low-speed:** 6 × I²C, 10 × UART, 4 × SPI, 16 × PWM, 4 × SARADC (10-bit, 0–1.8 V), up to 142 GPIO (RK3566 Brief, p. 2; HDG 1.1, p. 59).
- **IO power domains:** 10 domains, PMUIO0–2 and VCCIO1–7. PMUIO0 and PMUIO1 are fixed. PMUIO2 and VCCIO1, 3–7 need the software setting to match the supply. VCCIO2 is set by FLASH_VOL_SEL (IO Domain Configuration guide V1.0.2, p. 2).
- **Layout rules per interface:** in the [board research note `2026-10-rk3566-layout-rules.md`](https://github.com/opendap-project/board/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-rk3566-layout-rules.md) (HSPCB 1.0).

## Design notes for the T1

- **PMUIO1 is 3.3 V only** (2.97–3.63 V; DS 1.1, p. 52). PMUIO2 and VCCIO1–7 accept 1.8 or 3.3 V. The T1 sets PMUIO2 (I2C0/1/2) to 1.8 V and VCCIO5 (faceplate) to 3.3 V (BOM v1 §0 item 7).
- **LPDDR4X needs both 1.10 V (DDR_VDDQ, VCC_DDR) and 0.60 V (DDR_VDDQL)** at the SoC (HDG 1.1, p. 23). The 0.54–0.66 V window (DS 1.1, p. 52) drives the TPS7A1006 choice (line 9); VCC_DDR at 1.1 V comes from the PMIC. HSPCB 1.0 asks for at least 8 vias on the CPU-side VCC_DDR balls and at least 6 on VCC0V6_DDR (p. 30).
- **Ambient range 0–80 °C** (DS 1.1, p. 53) is narrower than the industrial ranges of the support parts; it bounds the T1 thermal design.
- **Audio clocks:** the CS43198 DACs take their MCLK from the dedicated oscillators (lines 20, 21, ADR 0010), not from an RK3566 PLL. The I2S blocks support master and slave (HDG 1.1, p. 69).
- **Several T1 audio balls sit in the 0.4 mm ring.** I2S1_M1 has SDO0 on AN2, SDO2 on AG2, SDO3 on AD1, SDI0/1 on AM2/AM1, SCLK_RX on AH2 and LRCK_TX on AP1. MCLK, SCLK_TX, LRCK_RX, SDO1 and SDI2/3 are core balls (DS 1.1, pp. 23–30). Their escape follows the ring rules in the board research note.
- **Decoupling under the SoC** puts 0201 and 0402 capacitors on the back side under the package (HDG 1.1, pp. 36–40). The board therefore needs back-side placement under the SoC, which the height zones must allow.
- **Thermal guidance** (HDG 1.1, pp. 92–93):
  - Place the PMIC 20–50 mm from the SoC.
  - Join the GND balls in a "#" pattern of 10 mil (0.254 mm) traces on the top layer.
  - Give every GND ball its own via (at worst one via per 1.5 balls), over an adjacent ground plane.
  - Connect the decoupling-capacitor ground pads under the SoC solid, without thermal reliefs.
