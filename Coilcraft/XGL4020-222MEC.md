## Summary

The 2.2 µH member of Coilcraft's XGL4020 shielded composite-core inductors, AEC-Q200 qualified, with soft saturation (Doc 1529 p. 1). In the T1 it is the inductor of the LMR33630C inverting buck-boost that makes the −7 V amplifier pre-rail from VSYS (board BOM v1.6 line 117). It is the part the board output-stage simulation used (§5.2).

## Absolute maximum ratings

As [XGL4020-102MEC](XGL4020-102MEC.md) (same series, Doc 1529 p. 1): ambient −40 to +125 °C with 40 °C rise at Irms, part ≤ +165 °C, operating voltage 80 V (note 7), three 40 s reflows at +260 °C, MSL 1.

## Power by mode

Passive part. DCR loss at the 0.78 A LDO-limit case (average inductor current ≈ 1.9 A at VSYS 6.0 V, est.): ≈ 0.08 W.

## Pinout and package

## Design notes for the T1

- **Operating points** (board output-stage simulation §5.2, VSYS 6.0 V): 1.80 A peak at the full-scale sine = 29 % of 6.2 A (41 % of 4.4 A); 2.13 A at the 0.78 A LDO-limit case = 34 % (48 %).
- **Fault:** the LMR33630 maximum high-side limit (5.05 A) is below the 6.2 A 30 % point, so a fault saturates it softly. With the XAL4020-222MEC (5.6 A) the same fault sits at 90 % of Isat.
