## Summary

The XGL4020-152MEC is the 1.5 µH member of Coilcraft's XGL4020 shielded composite-core power inductors, AEC-Q200 qualified, with low DCR, low AC loss and soft saturation (Doc 1529 p. 1). In the T1 it is the inductor of the TPS62136 pre-regulator that makes VSYS_LV (3.8 V) from the 2S VSYS (board BOM v1.5 line 114). TI's TPS62136 list (SLVSDV2 Table 3) names the XEL4020-152ME, not this part; the XGL4020-152 has the same body and land pattern and higher current ratings, and the XEL4020-152MEC is kept as its second source.

## Absolute maximum ratings

As [XGL4020-102MEC](XGL4020-102MEC.md) (same series, Doc 1529 p. 1): ambient −40 to +125 °C with 40 °C rise at Irms, part temperature ≤ +165 °C, storage −55 to +165 °C (component) and −55 to +80 °C (tape and reel), operating voltage 80 V (note 7), three 40 s reflows at +260 °C, MSL 1.

## Design notes for the T1

- **TPS62136 requirement** (SLVSDV2 §10.1.3, Eq. 9–10): 1.5 µH nominal (0.7–2.9 µH effective), saturation current about 20 % above IL(max), with ΔIL(max) = VIN(max) / L(min) × 100 ns. Eq. 10 understates the ripple of this converter (see the next item).
- **Operating point (corrected in board BOM v1.6, [boost and MaskROM simulation](https://github.com/opendap-project/board/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-boost-and-maskrom-simulation.md) §4.2):** SLVSDV2 Eq. 10 (100 ns) understates the ripple; the on-time of Eq. 1 (250 ns × VIN / (VIN − VOUT)) gives ΔIL = 250 ns × VIN / L, and TI's TPS62136 model agrees. At 2 A, VSYS 9.9 V (OVP), L − 20 %: ΔIL = 2.06 A, **peak 3.03 A = 40 % of 7.5 A** (30 % drop), 57 % of 5.3 A (20 % drop); the converter's switch peak is 63 % of its 4.8 A minimum limit. The v1.5 figure (2.41 A) used Eq. 10.
- **Fault:** the TPS62136 maximum current limit (6.5 A dc, SLVSDV2 p. 6) stays below the 7.5 A Isat (30 % drop).
- **Loss:** 2.0² × 14.3 mΩ = 57 mW at 2 A.
- **Stock (2026-10-22):** LCSC C7417180 2,017; Digi-Key 0; no Mouser listing. XEL4020-152MEC: Mouser 145, LCSC 289. **Thin**; Coilcraft direct stock not read.
