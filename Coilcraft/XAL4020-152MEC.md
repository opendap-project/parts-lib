- **Status:** Partly UNVERIFIED

## Summary

The XAL4020-152MEC is a 1.5 µH member of Coilcraft's XAL4020 shielded composite-core power inductors, AEC-Q200 qualified, with soft saturation (Doc 806 p. 1). In the T1 it is the inductor of the TPS61088 5.1 V boost (BOM line 91) that feeds HAT_5V and the USB-C VBUS source through eFuses #A and #C. Ordering code: `ME` = ±20 % with tin-silver over copper terminations, `C` = 7″ machine-ready reel (Doc 806 p. 1, note 1).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Requirement from the BOM:** 1.5 µH, Isat ≥ 6 A (BOM line 92). Isat 7.1 A typ meets it. The value and current come from the TPS61088 datasheet example, which is **not downloaded** (TI returns HTTP 404 for its datasheet URL on 2026-10-22), so the requirement itself is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Boost input current (calculation):** IIN = VOUT × IOUT / (VIN × η). Worst hardware case, both eFuses at ILIM (1.5 A + 1.5 A) at 5.1 V from VSYS = 3.5 V with η = 0.9: 15.3 W / 3.15 W·A⁻¹ = 4.86 A DC, plus half the ripple. On adapter power only HAT_5V can be sourced: 2.43 A. On battery the software budget leaves about 0.7 A at 5.1 V: about 1.1 A input (power tree §4.6).
- **Thermal:** at 4.86 A the inductor is near its 20 °C-rise Irms (5.2 A); DCR loss is 4.86² × 23.60 mΩ = 0.56 W worst case (calculation). The 3 A combined case is a fault-bounded ceiling, not a normal operating point.
- **Isat definition:** 30 % inductance drop (Doc 806 p. 1, note 5); check the TPS61088 switch current limit against the 1.5 µH L-vs-current curve (Doc 806 p. 2) once its datasheet is available.
- **Orientation:** start lead (dash) on the boost SW node (Doc 806 p. 4).
- **Height:** 2.1 mm maximum (Doc 806 p. 4).
