## Summary

The XAL4020-152MEC is a 1.5 µH member of Coilcraft's XAL4020 shielded composite-core power inductors, AEC-Q200 qualified, with soft saturation (Doc 806 p. 1). In the T1 it is the inductor of the TPS61089 5.1 V boost (BOM v1.1 line 91, [TPS61089RNRR](../Texas-Instruments/TPS61089RNRR.md)) that feeds HAT_5V and the USB-C VBUS source through eFuses #A and #C. Ordering code: `ME` = ±20 % with tin-silver over copper terminations, `C` = 7″ machine-ready reel (Doc 806 p. 1, note 1).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Requirement from the BOM:** 1.5 µH, Isat ≥ 6 A (BOM v1.1 line 92). Isat 7.1 A typ meets it. 1.5 µH is inside the TPS61089 inductance range of 0.47–10 µH effective (SLVSD38C p. 5, §7.3). TI asks for an inductor whose saturation current is above the set current limit (SLVSD38C p. 16, §9.2.2.5).
- **Operating peak (calculation, BOM v1.1 line 92 and power tree §4.6):** design output 1.6 A at 5.1 V, worst-case VIN = 3.0 V (VSYS follows VBAT on battery, so it can sit below 3.5 V), η = 0.88, fSW = 1 MHz. IDC = 5.1 × 1.6 / (3.0 × 0.88) = 3.1 A (SLVSD38C Eq. 6); ripple 0.96 A peak to peak with the power tree's D = 0.48, so the peak is 3.1 + 0.48 ≈ 3.6 A. With the datasheet's Eq. 7 (no η) and TI's advice to use L − 30 % (SLVSD38C p. 15), the ripple is 3.0 × 2.1 / (1.05 µH × 1 MHz × 5.1) = 1.18 A and the peak 3.7 A. Both sit below the 7.1 A Isat.
- **Fault bound:** RILIM = 200 kΩ sets the TPS61089 peak switch limit to 5.15 A typ (SLVSD38C Eq. 2). The BOM gives 4.6–5.7 A from the ±10 % spread characterised at 127 kΩ (SLVSD38C p. 6); the spread at 200 kΩ is **UNVERIFIED**. Isat 7.1 A covers the 5.7 A upper figure. From the 1.5 µH L-vs-current curve, inductance falls to roughly 1.2 µH near 5–6 A (read from the plot, Doc 806 p. 2; approximate).
- **Normal loads:** on adapter power only HAT_5V is sourced (eFuse #A ILIM 1.5 A); on battery HAT_5V and USB-C source together are budgeted to ≈ 0.4 A at 5.1 V, about 0.8 A input at 3.0 V (power tree §4.6, calculation).
- **Thermal:** at the 3.6 A design peak the RMS current is about 3.1 A, under the 5.2 A Irms for a 20 °C rise; DCR loss ≈ 3.1² × 23.60 mΩ = 0.23 W worst case (calculation, DCR max from Doc 806 p. 1). Core loss is not included (Coilcraft web tool, not opened).
- **Orientation:** start lead (dash) on the boost SW node (Doc 806 p. 4).
- **Height:** 2.1 mm maximum (Doc 806 p. 4).
