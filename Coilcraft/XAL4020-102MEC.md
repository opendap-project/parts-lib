# Coilcraft XAL4020-102MEC

- **Function:** shielded power inductor, 1.0 µH ±20 %, composite core, 4 × 4 × 2.1 mm
- **T1 use:** board BOM v1 line 40 (Power), status Proposed
- **Datasheet:** Shielded Power Inductors – XAL40xx, Document 806 (revised 02/25/26), <https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/6adcb47d-8b55-416c-976e-1e22e0d2848c/xal4000.pdf>
- **Package:** 4.0 × 4.0 mm SMD, 2.1 mm maximum height, two terminals

## Summary

The XAL4020-102MEC is a 1.0 µH member of Coilcraft's XAL4020 shielded composite-core power inductors, AEC-Q200 qualified, with soft saturation (Doc 806 p. 1). In the T1 it is the inductor of the BQ25895 buck charger (BOM line 39), which the charger datasheet asks to be 1 µH (BOM line 40 note, SLUSC88C §9). Ordering code: `ME` = ±20 % with tin-silver over copper terminations, `C` = 7″ machine-ready reel (Doc 806 p. 1, note 1).

## Design notes for the T1

- **Requirement from the BOM:** 1 µH, Isat ≥ 5 A (BOM line 40, from the BQ25895 datasheet §9). Isat 8.7 A typ (−30 % inductance point) meets it.
- **Current in the T1:** the inductor carries the charger's switched current, at most the VSYS + charge current on adapter power (VSYS rated 3.5 A on adapter, power tree §3). At 3.5 A DC the DCR loss is 3.5² × 14.60 mΩ = 0.18 W worst case (calculation); this is below the 20 °C-rise Irms of 6.7 A.
- **Isat definition:** Coilcraft's Isat is a 30 % drop (soft saturation, Doc 806 p. 1, note 5). Check the BQ25895's own current limit against the L-vs-current curve (Doc 806 p. 2, 1.0 µH plot), not only the Isat figure.
- **Orientation:** put the start (short) lead, marked by the dash on the body, on the charger SW node (Doc 806 p. 4).
- **Height:** 2.1 mm maximum (Doc 806 p. 4); check it against the case stack-up.
