## Summary

The XGL4020-102MEC is the 1.0 µH member of Coilcraft's XGL4020 shielded composite-core power inductors, AEC-Q200 qualified, with low DCR, low AC loss and soft saturation (Doc 1529 p. 1). TI lists the XGL4020-102ME as a recommended inductor for the TPS62903 (SLVSES3A Table 8-4) and uses it in the TPS62903 typical application (Table 8-1). In the T1 it is the inductor of both TPS62903 bucks: HAT_3V3 (line 7) and the 5.1 V HAT/USB-C source (line 92). Ordering code: `ME` = ±20 % with tin-silver over copper terminations, `C` = 7″ machine-ready reel (Doc 1529 p. 1, note 1).

## Design notes for the T1

- **TPS62903 requirement** (SLVSES3A §8.2.2.4): nominal 1 µH at 2.5 MHz, saturation current about 20 % above IL(max).
- **5.1 V rail (line 92):** 1.6 A out, VIN 8.8 V, L − 20 %: ΔIL = 1.07 A, peak 2.14 A = 24 % of the 8.8 A Isat (30 % drop), 34 % of 6.3 A (20 % drop). DCR loss 1.6² × 9.0 mΩ = 23 mW.
- **HAT_3V3 (line 7):** 1.15 A eFuse ceiling, VIN 8.8 V: peak 1.67 A = 19 % of 8.8 A.
- **Fault:** the TPS62903 maximum high-side limit (5.5 A) plus the 50 ns overshoot is ≈ 5.7 A at VIN 8.8 V, 65 % of 8.8 A, so a short does not drive it into hard saturation.
- **Stock (2026-10-22):** LCSC C5357696 1,146; Digi-Key 0; no Mouser listing found. Two per board. Coilcraft direct stock not read. Second source: XAL4020-102MEC (LCSC 7,672).
- **Height:** 2.10 mm maximum; check against the case stack-up.
