## Summary

The XFL4015-471MEC is the 0.47 µH member of Coilcraft's XFL4015 shielded composite-core inductors: 1.5 mm profile, very low DCR, soft saturation, AEC-Q200 qualified (Doc 769-1). Ordering code: `ME` = ±20 % with RoHS tin-silver over copper terminations, `C` = 7″ machine-ready reel of 1000 (Doc 769-1, note 1). BOM v1.3 lists it as the second source on line 106 for the TPS62826 3.3 V system buck inductor, from TI's recommended list.

## Design notes for the T1

- **Saturation against the converter limit:** Isat at 10 % drop (3.5 A) is below both the 3.7 A minimum and the 5.0 A maximum TPS62826 switch limit (BOM line 6). At 5.0 A the drop lies between 10 % and 20 % (Isat 20 % = 5.4 A, Doc 769-1). The 2.25 A design peak (BOM line 6) is below the 10 % point. The BOM line 106 figures match the datasheet.
- **DCR:** 8.36 mΩ max, lower than the Würth part's 14 mΩ max.
- **Not land-compatible with the Würth 744373240047 as drawn.** Würth recommends 1.5 × 2.4 mm pads at 3.7 mm centre-to-centre (5.2 mm outer extent); Coilcraft recommends 0.98 × 3.4 mm pads at 2.37 mm (3.35 mm extent). A land pattern that fits both parts is **UNVERIFIED**; BOM line 106 already marks land compatibility as unverified.
- **Height:** 1.60 mm max, lower than the Würth part (2.0 mm max).
- **Orientation:** start (short) lead to the TPS62826 SW node (Doc 769-2).
